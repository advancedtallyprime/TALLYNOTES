Concept

Inventory in Tally Prime means managing your physical goods/stock. This section allows you to create:

  • Stock Groups (categories of items)
  • Units of Measure (how you measure items)
  • Stock Items (actual products you buy and sell)

This feature is used when your business deals with goods (trading or manufacturing).

Use Case & Real Life Practice

  • Use Case: Businesses that buy and sell products need to track stock quantity, value, and movement.
  • Real Life Examples:
    • A mobile shop tracks different models of phones.
    • A grocery store tracks rice, oil, spices.
    • A stationery shop tracks notebooks, pens, files.

Benefit: Tally automatically reduces stock when you sell and increases when you purchase.

Step-by-Step Guidance (Verbal Instructions)

1. Create Stock Group

  1. Gateway of Tally → Create → Stock Group
  2. Enter:
    • Name: e.g., “Mobiles”, “Groceries”, “Stationery”
    • Under: Primary or another group
  3. Accept.

2. Create Unit of Measure

  1. Gateway → Create → Unit
  2. Enter:
    • Symbol: Nos, Kg, Box, Pcs, Dozen
    • Formal Name: Numbers, Kilograms, etc.
  3. Accept.

3. Create Stock Item

  1. Gateway → Create → Stock Item
  2. Enter:
    • Name: e.g., “Redmi Note 12”
    • Under: Select proper Stock Group
    • Units: Select unit (Nos)
    • Rate:
      • Purchase Price (optional)
      • Sales Price (optional)
    • Opening Balance: Quantity + Rate (if any)
  3. Accept.

Example

Stock Group: Electronics
Unit: Nos

Stock Items:

  1. Dell Laptop
    • Under: Electronics
    • Units: Nos
    • Purchase Price: ₹38,000
    • Sales Price: ₹45,000
    • Opening Qty: 5 Nos @ ₹37,000
  2. Wireless Mouse
    • Under: Electronics
    • Units: Nos
    • Purchase Price: ₹350
    • Sales Price: ₹550
