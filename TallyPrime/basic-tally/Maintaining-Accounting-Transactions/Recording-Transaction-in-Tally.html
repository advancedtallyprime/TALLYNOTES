Concept

In Tally Prime, every business activity (buying, selling, receiving money, paying money, adjustments, etc.) is recorded through Vouchers.

A voucher is like a digital receipt or slip. Tally follows the Double Entry System — every transaction affects at least two accounts.

This is the most important practical part of Tally.

Use Case & Real Life Practice

  • Use Case: Recording daily business activities so that you can generate accurate reports (Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Stock Summary, GST reports).
  • Real Life Examples:
    • A shopkeeper records cash sales, credit purchases, payments to suppliers, and money received from customers.
    • A professional maintains records of fees received and expenses paid.

Key Principle: “Every Debit must have a corresponding Credit”.

Main Types of Vouchers (Quick Overview)

Voucher Type Shortcut Purpose
Receipt F6 Money received
Payment F5 Money paid out
Contra F4 Transfer between Cash & Bank
Sales F8 Sale of goods/services
Purchase F9 Purchase of goods
Debit Note Ctrl + F9 Return to supplier
Credit Note Ctrl + F8 Return from customer
Journal F7 Adjustments & Provisions

Step-by-Step General Process for Recording Any Voucher

  1. Open your company in Tally Prime.
  2. At Gateway of Tally, press the shortcut key of the voucher (e.g., F8 for Sales).
  3. Enter Date (use F2 to change date).
  4. Select proper Ledger accounts.
  5. If inventory is involved, select Stock Items, Quantity, and Rate.
  6. Enter Narration (explanation of transaction) — very important for understanding later.
  7. Press Accept or Y to save.

Pro Tip: Always check the Balance shown at the bottom before accepting.

Practice Set (Overview)

  • Create at least 2 transactions of each voucher type (we will cover in detail in upcoming topics).
  • After recording 8–10 transactions, view:
    • Day Book (Display → Day Book)
    • Trial Balance
    • Profit & Loss Account
    • Balance Sheet

Connection to Next Topics

We will now study each voucher type in detail, starting with Receipt Voucher. Every voucher will use the Ledgers and Stock Items you created earlier.
