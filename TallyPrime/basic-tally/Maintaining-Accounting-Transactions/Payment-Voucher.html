1. Core Concept

Payment Voucher (F5): Used to record all outgoing cash or bank payments made by the business.

Common Use Cases:

  • Clearing outstanding bills/dues of creditors/suppliers (Bill-by-Bill settlement against Purchase Invoices).
  • Paying operational expenses (e.g., Rent, Salaries, Electricity Bills).
  • Purchasing fixed assets for cash/bank.

Accounting Impact:

  • Debit → Receiver / Supplier / Expense Account
  • Credit → Cash / Bank Account
Educational Mode Payment Voucher Tip:
  • Press F5 for Payment voucher.
  • Set voucher date to 1-4 or 2-4 (F2).
  • In Educational Mode, bank ledger payments and cash payments operate with complete accuracy.
  • When settling bills with Against Reference (Agst Ref), verify that purchase bill was also recorded on 1st or 2nd.

2. How to Record Payment Vouchers in TallyPrime

  1. Go to Gateway of Tally → Vouchers → Press F5 (Payment).
  2. Select Mode: Press Ctrl + H to choose between Single-Entry Mode (easiest for cash/bank payments) or Double-Entry Mode (Debit/Credit).
  3. Select Payment Source: Choose Cash or Bank Account in the Account field.
  4. Select Party: Under Particulars, select the supplier ledger (e.g., Anil InfoTech).
  5. Bill-wise Settlement:
    • In the Bill-wise Details pop-up, select Agst Ref (Against Reference).
    • Select the corresponding Purchase Invoice Number (e.g., P-106).
    • Enter the Amount being paid.
  6. Fill in Narration: Being amount paid.
  7. Press Ctrl + A to save.

4. Checking Effect & Report Verification

To View Statistics & Payment Count:

  • Navigation Path:
    Gateway of Tally → Display More Reports → Statements of Accounts → Statistics → Select Payment
    (Shortcut: Press Alt + G → Type Statistics → Drill down into Payment)

To Check / Delete / Modify Payment Entries:

  • Navigation Path:
    Gateway of Tally → Display More Reports → Account Books → Payment Register
    (Shortcut: Press Alt + G → Type Payment Register)
  • Action: Select the month → Press Enter on the transaction to view or edit details. Press Alt + D inside the voucher to delete it.
