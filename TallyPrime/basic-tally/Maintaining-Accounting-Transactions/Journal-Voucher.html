1. Core Concept

Journal Voucher (F7): An adjustment voucher used for non-cash and non-bank transactions. It records entries that do not directly involve receiving or paying liquid cash/bank funds at the moment of entry.

Common Use Cases:

  • Credit purchases of Fixed Assets (e.g., buying furniture or machinery on credit).
  • Adjustment entries (e.g., depreciation, outstanding expenses, prepaid expenses, accrued income).
  • Rectification of accounting errors.
  • Opening balances and year-end closing entries.
Golden Rule of Accounting:
Debit (Dr) → Receiver / What comes in / Expenses & Losses
Credit (Cr) → Giver / What goes out / Incomes & Gains
Educational Mode Journal Voucher Tip:
  • Press F7 for Journal voucher (non-cash adjustments, provisions, asset credit purchases).
  • Remember to keep adjustment dates on 1-4, 2-4, 31-5, 31-7, 31-10, 31-12, 31-1, or 31-3.
  • Year-end depreciation and closing provisions should be dated 31-Mar-2026 (F2).

Insurance Expense A/c Indirect Expenses Regular expense
Bad Debts A/c Indirect Expenses Irrecoverable customer debt

3. How to Record Journal Vouchers in TallyPrime

  1. Go to Gateway of Tally → Vouchers → Press F7 (Journal).
  2. Press Ctrl + H to select Double Entry mode (By/To or Dr/Cr).
  3. First Line: Select the Debit (Dr) ledger and enter the amount.
  4. Second Line: Select the Credit (Cr) ledger and enter the corresponding amount.
  5. Enter a clear Narration describing the adjustment.
  6. Press Ctrl + A to save.

5. Checking, Deleting & Modifying Journal Vouchers

To View Statistics & Journal Count:

  • Navigation Path:
    Gateway of Tally → Display More Reports → Statements of Accounts → Statistics → Select Journal
    (Shortcut: Press Alt + G → Type Statistics → Drill down into Journal)

To Check / Delete / Modify Journal Entries:

  • Navigation Path:
    Gateway of Tally → Display More Reports → Account Books → Journal Register
    (Shortcut: Press Alt + G → Type Journal Register)
  • Action: Select the month → Press Enter on the voucher to view or edit details. Press Alt + D inside the voucher to delete it.
