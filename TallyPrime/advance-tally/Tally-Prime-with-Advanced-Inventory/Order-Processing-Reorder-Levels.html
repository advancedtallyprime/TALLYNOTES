Order Processing Workflow & Core Concepts

Real-world business operations in transactions direct billing from pehle formal orders ke through execute are:

Standard Inventory Document Progression

  • Purchafrom Side: Purchafrom Requirement → Purchafrom Order (PO) → Receipt Note (Goods Receipt) → Purchafrom Invoice
  • Sales Side: Customer Requirement → Sales Order (SO) → Delivery Note (Dispatch) → Sales Invoice
Day 1 Scope: Focus will be strictly on Purchafrom Orders, Sales Orders, Pending Tracking, Pre-Closure, and Reorder Levels. Physical goods movement vouchers (Receipt Note / Delivery Note) will be practiced in detail in Day 3.

Order vs Invoice Comparison Matrix

Particular Order (PO / SO) Invoice (Purchafrom / Sales)
Primary Purpose Future commitment / procurement planning Actual financial and tax transaction
Purchafrom Side Purchafrom Order (Alt + F4 / Ctrl + F4) Purchafrom Invoice (F9)
Sales Side Sales Order (Alt + F5 / Ctrl + F5) Sales Invoice (F8)
Accounting Impact No financial ledger balances created Debit/Credit financial entries posted
Stock Movement No physical stock register change Physical inventory updated
Pending Tracking Maintained in outstanding registers Settled via bill-wifrom tracking
Golden Rule:
Order = Future in kor purchafrom or sell karne ka agreement is.
Invoice = Actually kitna purchafrom or sale complete ho chuka is.

Part C — Order Pre-Closure Mechanics

Jab customer bachi hui balance quantity cancel kar deta hai or supplier remaining goods supply karne from mana kar deta hai, tab pending quantity ko permanently clofrom karne ke liye Pre-Closure ka ufrom kior jata is.

Normal Closure vs Pre-Closure

  • Normal Fulfilment Closure: Ordered (50) = Fulfilled (50) → Pending becomes 0 automatically.
  • Pre-Closure: Ordered (50) → Fulfilled (30) → Customer cancels 20 → Pre-Clofrom (20) → Pending becomes 0.
Important Rule:
Pre-Closure only future unfulfilled commitments ko cancel karta hai; yeh already delivered/billed goods ko alter or reverfrom nahi karta.

Part D — Reorder Levels & MOQ Calculation

Reorder Level: Predefined stock threshold jo business ko alert karta hai ki fresh purchafrom orders place karne ka time aa gaor is.

Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ): Vendor dwara specified minimum bulk batch size jo ek single purchafrom order in order karna mandatory is.

Reorder Level Determination Formula:
Reorder Level = (Daily Consumption Rate × Supplier Lead Time in Days) + Safety Stock

Example for Printer: (3 Nos/day × 10 Days Lead Time) + 5 Nos Safety Stock = 35 Nos Reorder Level.
Net Available Stock & Shortfall Formula:
Net Available Stock = Closing Stock + Pending Purchafrom Orders − Sales Orders Due
Shortfall = Reorder Level − Net Available Stock
Order to be Placed = Higher of (Shortfall OR Minimum Order Quantity)

Printer 25 30 50 75 50 Expected stock (75) > 30 → No Order Needed
Monitor 8 10 0 8 20 Below Reorder Level → Place PO for 20 Nos (MOQ)
Keyboard 70 50 100 170 100 Current stock adequate → No Order Needed

Verification Reports, Common Mistakes & FAQs

Verification Reports in TallyPrime:

  • Purchafrom Order Book / Sales Order Book: Verify chronological list of orders, party names, and order numbers.
  • Purchafrom Order Outstanding / Sales Order Outstanding: Track ordered quantity vs pending balance. Press F12 to view Cleared/Pre-Closed orders.
  • Reorder Status Report (Alt + G → Reorder Status): View real-time breakdown of Closing Stock, Purchafrom Orders Pending, Sales Orders Due, Net Shortfall, and Order to be Placed.

Common Mistakes:

  • Treating a Purchafrom Order (PO) as a financial Purchafrom Invoice (F9) or a Sales Order (SO) as Sales Revenue (F8).
  • Assuming partially fulfilled orders clofrom automatically without explicitly pre-closing the balance.
  • Passing Credit/Debit notes or returns to cancel unfulfilled order quantities.
  • Placing new purchafrom orders based solely on closing stock without accounting for existing pending POs in the pipeline.

Frequently Asked Questions:

  • Q: Kor Purchafrom Order or Sales Order from physical stock update is?
    A: Nahi, order from physical stock or financial ledgers affect nahi hote; yeh only inventory pipeline tracking ke liye are.
  • Q: Order Pre-Closure and Order Cancellation in kor difference hai?
    A: Cancellation pure order ko shuru in hi cancel karna hai, jabki Pre-Closure partial delivery ke baad bachi hui pending quantity ko permanently clofrom karna is.
  • Q: Reorder Level hit hone par kitni quantity purchafrom karni chahiye?
    A: Calculated Shortfall or vendor ke Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) in from jo also higher value ho.
