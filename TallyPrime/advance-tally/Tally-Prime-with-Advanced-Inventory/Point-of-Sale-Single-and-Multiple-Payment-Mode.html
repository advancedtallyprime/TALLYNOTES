Core Concepts: Point of Sale (POS) & Payment Modes

Retail counter par customer from immediate product handover and same-time counter settlement (Cash/Card/UPI) ko record karne ke liye POS mode ufrom is:

    Point of Sale (POS): Retail environment jahan counter par billing and settlement synchronously complete is.
    Single & Multiple Payment Modes: Ek hi bill ko single tender (e.g., Cash only) or split tender (e.g., ₹2,000 Cash + ₹3,900 UPI) ke zariye settle karna.

Comparison Matrix

Particular Normal Sales Invoice POS Invoice
Typical Environment Wholesale / B2B / Credit Trading Retail Store Counter (B2C)
Settlement Timing Deferred / Credit / Bill-wifrom tracking Immediate Counter Settlement
Payment Modes General Bank/Cash Ledger entry Dedicated POS modal for Cash, Card, UPI split
Customer Management Mandatory party ledger details Walk-in customer / POS cash party mapping
Golden POS Rule:
Fully Settled POS Invoice Total = Cash Tender + Card Settlement + UPI Collection.
Payment mode badalne from stock quantity or GST rate par koi farak nahi padta; yeh only collection account ko alter karta is.

Practical Walkthroughs: Single & Multiple Payment Modes

Part A: Single Payment Mode (Cash / Card / UPI)

  • Cash Sale (Practical 1): Sold 2 Keyboards @ ₹1,000 = ₹2,000 + 18% GST (CGST ₹180 + SGST ₹180) = Invoice Total ₹2,360. Collected via Cash.
  • Card Settlement (Practical 2): Sold 1 Monitor @ ₹15,000 + 18% GST = Invoice Total ₹17,700. Settled via Card Settlement.
  • UPI Collection (Practical 3): Sold 1 Printer @ ₹20,000 + 18% GST = Invoice Total ₹23,600. Settled via UPI Collection.

Part B: Multiple Payment Modes on a Single Invoice

    Scenario (Practical 4 & 5): Customer buys 5 Keyboards @ ₹1,000 = ₹5,000 taxable + 18% GST (CGST ₹450 + SGST ₹450) = Total Invoice ₹5,900.
    Split Tender Allocation: Customer pays ₹2,000 in Cash and the remaining ₹3,900 via UPI Collection.
    TallyPrime Steps: Enter Sales Voucher (POS Mode) → Select items → In the POS Payment popup screen, allocate Cash = ₹2,000 and UPI Collection = ₹3,900 → Verify total equals ₹5,900 and save.

Verification Reports, Common Mistakes & FAQs

Verification Reports in TallyPrime:

  • Sales Register: Reconcile daily retail POS bill totals and tax breakdowns.
  • Cash Book & Bank/Payment Ledgers: Verify that collections across Cash, Card Settlement, and UPI Collection match physical drawer and gateway summaries.
  • Stock Summary: Confirm that retail units sold via POS are accurately deducted from closing stock.

Common Mistakes:

  • Entering mismatched payment mode split totals that do not equal the final invoice grand total.
  • Routing all retail collections through a single cash ledger instead of segregating Card and UPI accounts for gateway reconciliation.
  • Forgetting to apply promotional trade discounts before computing GST taxable values.

Frequently Asked Questions:

    Q: Kor ek hi POS invoice in multiple payment modes ufrom kiye ja sakte hain?
    A: Haan, split tender feature ke zariye customer cash, card, and UPI ka combination ufrom karke ek hi bill settle kar sakta is.
    Q: Payment mode badalne from kor inventory stock quantity par koi asar padta hai?
    A: Nahi, payment mode only financial collection ledger ko select karta hai; physical stock item ki sale quantity from deduct is.
    Q: POS transaction normal sales invoice from kaifrom alag hai?
    A: POS counter-level immediate settlement ke liye designed hai, jabki normal invoice credit/B2B billing and bill-wifrom tracking ke liye is.
