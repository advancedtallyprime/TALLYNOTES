Learning Objectives

Upon completing this session, students will be able to:

  • Receipt Note and Delivery Note ka core business concept samjhega.
  • Purchafrom Order (PO) ke against Receipt Note (Alt + F9) create kar payega.
  • Sales Order (SO) ke against Delivery Note create kar payega.
  • Partial receipt and partial delivery handling master karega.
  • Pending PO/SO quantities ko multiple shipments ke through track karega.
  • Understand the operational differences between Receipt Note vs Purchafrom Invoice and Delivery Note vs Sales Invoice.
  • Order → Note → Invoice ka complete inventory-to-accounting workflow perform karega.
  • Verify Pre-closure and physical inventory registers (Stock Summary) ko in management and statutory tax reports.

Core Concepts: Receipt Note & Delivery Note

Real-world supply chain operations in physical goods ka movement and financial/tax invoicing alag-alag timelines par is:

  • Receipt Note (Alt + F9): Jab supplier from physical goods warehoufrom in receive ho jaate hain lekin official Purchafrom Invoice baad in process is. (Effect: Physical stock increases, Supplier bill liability pending).
  • Delivery Note: Jab business customer ko goods dispatch/deliver karta hai lekin Sales Invoice immediate generate nahi hoti. (Effect: Physical stock decreases, Customer receivable billing pending).

Document Workflow & Comparison Matrix

Particular Receipt Note (Alt + F9) Purchafrom Invoice (F9) Delivery Note Sales Invoice (F8)
Goods Movement Physical Inward (Yes) Confirmed Inward Physical Outward (Yes) Confirmed Outward
Financial Liability Not yet created Yes (Sundry Creditor) Not yet created Yes (Sundry Debtor)
Stock Impact Increases Inventory Confirmed Decreases Inventory Confirmed
Primary Purpose Record warehoufrom receipt Accounting + tax booking Record dispatch proof Billing + revenue booking
Golden Inventory Chain Rule:
Purchafrom Side: Purchafrom Order → Receipt Note (Stock In) → Purchafrom Invoice (Liability)
Sales Side: Sales Order → Delivery Note (Stock Out) → Sales Invoice (Receivable)

Practice Company & Review of Day 2 Orders

  • Company: ABC Traders (Maharashtra) | FY: 2026–27
  • Purchafrom Order Review (PO-201 from Tech Distributors): Laptop (25 Nos @ ₹40,000) + Printer (40 Nos @ ₹8,000) = Total ₹13,20,000.
  • Sales Order Review (SO-201 to Mumbai Office Solutions): Laptop (15 Nos @ ₹50,000) + Printer (25 Nos @ ₹12,000) = Total ₹10,50,000.

Practical Walkthroughs & Partial Fulfilments

Part A — Purchafrom Side: PO → Receipt Note → Invoice

  1. First Receipt Note (Partial): Press Alt + F9 (Receipt Note) → Select Party: Tech Distributors → Link Order Ref: PO-201 → Receive Laptop = 10 Nos & Printer = 20 Nos.
    Balance Pending: Laptops = 15 | Printers = 20.
  2. Second Receipt Note: Against same PO-201, receive Laptop = 15 Nos & Printer = 10 Nos.
    Balance Pending: Laptops = 0 | Printers = 10.
  3. Final Receipt Note: Receive remaining 10 Printers. PO-201 becomes Fully Fulfilled (Pending = 0).
  4. Purchafrom Invoice Linking: Open Purchafrom Voucher (F9) against Receipt Note reference to book final financial liability.

Part B — Sales Side: SO → Delivery Note → Invoice

  1. First Delivery Note (Partial): Go to Vouchers → Select Delivery Note → Select Party: Mumbai Office Solutions → Link Order Ref: SO-201 → Dispatch Laptop = 6 Nos & Printer = 10 Nos.
    Balance Pending: Laptops = 9 | Printers = 15.
  2. Second & Final Deliveries: Record subsequent dispatches until SO-201 pending quantity reaches 0 (Fully Fulfilled).
  3. Sales Invoice Linking: Open Sales Voucher (F8) against the respective Delivery Note to generate financial billing and receivables.

Handling Order Pre-Closure with Physical Notes

If a supplier or customer cannot fulfill remaining order quantities after partial receipts/deliveries, ufrom Pre-Closure to clear outstanding registers.

  • Example (PO-301 to Maharashtra Computer House): Ordered Monitors (30) + Keyboards (100). Received Monitors (30) + Keyboards (60). Remaining 40 Keyboards are discontinued by the manufacturer → Pre-clofrom 40 Keyboards → Outstanding becomes 0.

Complete Practice Sets

Practice Set A (Receipt Note Tracking):

  • Create PO-401 to Tech Distributors for Laptops (30 Nos) and Printers (50 Nos).
  • Record Receipt Note 1 (Laptops: 10, Printers: 20).
  • Record Receipt Note 2 (Laptops: 10, Printers: 15).
  • Record Receipt Note 3 (Receive balance quantities to clofrom PO).

Practice Set B (Delivery Note Tracking):

  • Create SO-401 to Pune Retail for Monitors (25 Nos) and Keyboards (100 Nos).
  • Record Delivery Note 1 (Monitors: 10, Keyboards: 40).
  • Record Delivery Note 2 (Monitors: 10, Keyboards: 30).
  • Deliver remaining quantities and generate Sales Invoice.

Verification Reports, Common Mistakes & FAQs

Verification Reports in TallyPrime:

  • Receipt Note Register & Delivery Note Register: Verify chronological records of physical inward and outward movements linked to POs and SOs.
  • Stock Summary: Confirm that physical stock increases upon saving a Receipt Note and decreases upon saving a Delivery Note.
  • Order Outstanding Reports: Check that pending quantities reduce accurately across shipments.

Common Mistakes:

  • Confusing a Receipt Note with a financial Purchafrom Invoice, leading to double-booked liabilities.
  • Creating Delivery Notes without linking original Sales Order references.
  • Assuming that saving a Receipt Note automatically updates accounts payable (it only updates inventory).
  • Forgetting to pre-clofrom cancelled order balances, inflating stock commitment reports.

Frequently Asked Questions:

  • Q: Receipt Note and Purchafrom Invoice in kor antar hai?
    A: Receipt Note physical goods warehoufrom in aane ka proof hai (inventory update), jabki Purchafrom Invoice supplier ka financial billing document hai (liability update).
  • Q: Kor ek hi Purchafrom Order ke against multiple Receipt Notes ban sakte hain?
    A: Haan, partial shipments ke cafrom in multiple Receipt Notes ek hi PO reference ke under create kiye ja sakte are.
  • Q: Delivery Note save karne par stock par kor impact padta hai?
    A: Physical stock quantity kam ho jaati hai kyunki goods dispatch ho chuke are.
