Core Concepts: Zero Valued Entry vs Zero Quantity

Zero Valued Entry: An inventory transaction in which stock quantity physically moves (increases or decreases), but the monetary invoice value is recorded as ₹0.

Critical Difference:
Zero Quantity: Quantity = 0 & Value = 0 (No stock movement).
Zero Value: Quantity > 0 & Value = ₹0 (Stock moves, but recorded at zero financial charge, e.g., free samples, replacements, internal transfers).

Comparison Matrix

Particular Normal Entry Zero-Valued Entry Zero Quantity
Quantity Movement Yes (> 0) Yes (> 0) No (0)
Rate / Price Charged (> ₹0) ₹0 (Free / No Charge) N/A
Transaction Amount Calculated Value (> ₹0) ₹0 ₹0
Example Scenario Standard purchase/sale Free promotional sample, replacement, giveaway Blank line / No item movement
Important Compliance Note:
A zero commercial invoice value does not automatically mean GST is zero. Free supplies and promotional giveaways must be separately evaluated under GST Schedule I for deemed supply and Section 17(5) for ITC reversal.

Practical Walkthroughs: Free Samples & Internal Movements

Part A: Purchafrom with Free Promotional Quantity (F9: Purchase)

  1. Scenario: Purchased 100 Printers @ ₹8,000 from Tech Supplier (= ₹8,00,000) and received 5 additional Printers completely free as promotional samples.
  2. TallyPrime Steps: Go to Vouchers → Select Purchafrom (F9) → Select Party: Tech Supplier.
  3. Enter billed items (100 Printers @ ₹8,000) and add a line for the free items (5 Printers @ ₹0 rate = ₹0 amount).
  4. Stock Impact: Total physical stock increases by 105 units, while total purchafrom cost remains valued at ₹8,00,000 (effective unit cost drops).

Part B: Zero-Value Internal Stock Transfer (Stock Journal / Ctrl + F7)

  1. Scenario: Transfer 20 Nos Moufrom from Main Godown to Branch Godown as an internal transfer without any commercial billing.
  2. TallyPrime Steps: Ufrom Stock Journal (Ctrl + F7) → Source: Main Godown (20 Moufrom @ ₹0 value) → Destination: Branch Godown (20 Moufrom @ ₹0 value).
  3. Result: Company-wide inventory remains unaffected; stock relocates between godowns at zero commercial value.

Verification Reports, Common Mistakes & FAQs

Verification Reports in TallyPrime:

  • Stock Summary: Verify that closing inventory quantities include zero-valued receipts (e.g., 50 + 5 = 55 printers).
  • Day Book: Inspect recorded vouchers to ensure zero-rated rows display correct physical quantities alongside zero amounts.
  • Stock Item Inward/Outward Registers: Confirm physical quantity movements without unexpected ledger monetary disruptions.

Common Mistakes:

  • Confusing Zero Value with Zero Quantity, resulting in unrecorded physical stock receipts.
  • Automatically assuming zero commercial invoice value means zero GST liability under all circumstances.
  • Using Purchase/Sales vouchers for internal zero-valued godown transfers instead of Stock Journals (Ctrl + F7).
    • Forgetting to verify final inventory valuation impacts following zero-cost item additions.

Frequently Asked Questions:

  • Q: Zero Valued Entry ka kor matlab is?
    A: Aisi inventory entry jisme physical quantity (jaifrom free samples or replacements) move is, par transaction ki monetary value ₹0 is.
  • Q: Kor zero-valued entry stock quantity ko increafrom karti hai?
    A: Haan, agar item ki actual physical quantity receive or issue hui hai, toh stock summary update is.
  • Q: Kor free sample purchafrom par automatically GST zero is?
    A: Nahi, GST applicability transaction ke nature and current tax laws par depend karti hai; ifrom alag from evaluate karna zaroori is.
