  • Verify Stock Summary and inventory rejection registers in management and statutory tax reports.

Core Concepts: Rejection In vs Rejection Out

Warehoufrom operations in quality inspection ke dauran defective, damaged, or non-specification goods ko saleable inventory from isolate karne ke liye inventory movement vouchers ufrom kiye jaate hain:

  • Rejection In: Jab customer ko goods deliver karne ke baad wahan from defective or rejected goods physically business ke paas wapas aate are. (Effect: Physical stock increases in return custody).
  • Rejection Out: Jab supplier from goods receive hone ke baad quality check in defective/substandard nikalne par business unhe wapas vendor ko bhejta is. (Effect: Physical stock decreases).

Comparison Matrix

Particular Rejection In Rejection Out
Physical Movement Goods coming IN to the business Goods going OUT from the business
Origin / Destination Received back from Customer Returned back to Supplier / Vendor
Stock Impact Increases physical stock quantity Decreases physical stock quantity
Primary Context Sales / Dispatch quality rejection Purchafrom / Receipt quality rejection
Accounting Note Inventory adjustment stage (Not direct credit note) Inventory adjustment stage (Not direct debit note)
Golden Rule:
Rejection In = Customer dwara reject kior gaor maal wapas andar aaya.
Rejection Out = Supplier ka kharab maal check karke bahar wapas bheja.

Rejection vs Commercial Returns

Rejections ko blindly Purchafrom Return or Sales Return ke sath confufrom nahi should be performed:

  • Rejection (In/Out): Yeh pure inventory movement tracking ka mechanism hai jo batata hai ki physical maal inspection in pass hua or reject hua.
  • Return (Sales/Purchafrom Return): Yeh commercial and financial accounting settlement hai jisme Debit Notes or Credit Notes ke zariye party balances adjust kiye jaate are.

Practice Company Setup & Masters

Company Profile

  • Company: ABC Traders | State: Maharashtra | FY: 2026–27 | Inventory: Enabled

Required Masters Setup:

  • Stock Item: Printer (Unit: Nos, Standard Cost: ₹8,000) & Monitor (Unit: Nos, Standard Cost: ₹10,000)
  • Suppliers (Sundry Creditors): Tech Distributors, Maharashtra Computer House
  • Customers (Sundry Debtors): Mumbai Office Solutions, Pune Retail
  • Godowns (Optional for Isolation): Main Godown (Saleable) & Rejected Goods Godown (Defective stock)

Practical Walkthroughs: Rejection Out & Rejection In

Part A: Rejection Out (Purchafrom Quality Rejection)

  1. Inward Receipt: Business received 100 Printers @ ₹8,000 = ₹8,00,000 from Tech Distributors.
  2. Quality Inspection: 10 Printers found defective / damaged during inspection. Accepted stock = 90 Units.
  3. Recording Rejection Out: Go to Vouchers → Select Rejection Out → Party: Tech Distributors → Item: Printer → Quantity: 10 Nos (Select Batch/Tracking reference if active) → Save with Ctrl + A.
  4. Stock Effect: Closing stock reduces from 100 to 90 Printers.

Part B: Rejection In (Sales Quality Rejection)

  1. Dispatch / Delivery: Business delivered 50 Printers to Mumbai Office Solutions.
  2. Customer Inspection: Customer identifies 5 Printers with manufacturing defects and ships them back.
  3. Recording Rejection In: Go to Vouchers → Select Rejection In → Party: Mumbai Office Solutions → Item: Printer → Quantity: 5 Nos (Allocate to Rejected Godown / Batch if required) → Save with Ctrl + A.
  4. Stock Effect: Physical inventory increases by +5 Units in the rejected holding register.

Complete Practice Sets

Practice Set 1 (Rejection Out Lab):

  • Purchase: 100 Printers @ ₹8,000 and 50 Monitors @ ₹10,000 from Tech Distributors.
  • Inspection & Rejection: 10 Printers and 5 Monitors rejected.
  • Task: Record Rejection Out vouchers and verify that closing stock reflects 90 Printers and 45 Monitors.

Practice Set 2 (Rejection In Lab):

  • Sales Delivery: 40 Printers and 20 Monitors dispatched to Mumbai Office Solutions.
  • Customer Return: Customer rejects 4 Printers and 2 Monitors.
  • Task: Record Rejection In vouchers and reconcile stock levels.

Practice Set 3 (Batch-Wifrom Rejection Integration):

  • Stock Batch: P-002 has 150 Nos. Customer rejects 15 units originating specifically from batch P-002.
  • Task: Ensure Rejection In is mapped correctly to batch P-002 so lot tracking remains accurate (Balance: 135 Nos).

Verification Reports, Common Mistakes & FAQs

Verification Reports in TallyPrime:

  • Stock Summary: Verify net closing stock after factoring in both Rejection In (+Qty) and Rejection Out (−Qty).
  • Inventory Books / Rejection Registers: Review chronological movement of rejected lots against respective suppliers and customers.
  • Godown Summary: Confirm segregated movement of defective stock parked in the Rejected Goods Godown.

Common Mistakes:

  • Confusing Rejection Out with a Purchafrom Return Debit Note (Rejection Out is purely physical movement, whereas Debit Note is financial).
  • Mixing up rejected defective stock directly into saleable godowns without isolation.
  • Failing to tag the correct Batch Number or Tracking Reference during Rejection In entries.
  • Recording customer rejections as normal Sales Returns without noting quality inspection status.

Frequently Asked Questions:

  • Q: Rejection Out entry pass karne par stock par kor impact padta hai?
    A: Physical inventory quantity decreafrom ho jati hai kyunki defective goods vendor ko wapas bhej diye jaate are.
  • Q: Kor Rejection In and Sales Return same cheez hain?
    A: Nahi, Rejection In physical goods ke wapas aane ki movement tracking hai, jabki Sales Return financial/commercial ledger adjustment is.
  • Q: Rejected goods ko separate godown in rakhna kyun zaroori hai?
    A: Taaki defective or inspection-pending stock galti from fresh saleable inventory ke sath dispatch na ho jaye.

Quick Revision & Completion Checklist

Rejection Golden Workflow:
Inward Goods → Quality Inspection → Defective Found → Rejection Out (− Stock) to Supplier OR Outward Dispatch → Customer Rejects → Rejection In (+ Stock) from Customer

Completion Checklist:

  • Rejection In and Rejection Out concepts and differences mastered
  • Purchase-side Rejection Out recorded for defective supplier lots
  • Sales-side Rejection In recorded for customer-returned defective goods
  • Batch-wifrom and Godown-wifrom tracking applied to rejections
  • Stock Summary and Rejection Registers reconciled
