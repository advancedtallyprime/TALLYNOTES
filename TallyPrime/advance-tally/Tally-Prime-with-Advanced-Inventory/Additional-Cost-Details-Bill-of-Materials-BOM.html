Core Concepts: Additional Cost (Landed Cost) & BOM

Advanced inventory and manufacturing operations in goods ki proper costing and production planning ke liye do key tools ufrom are:

  • Additional Cost Details: Purchafrom invoice ke basic bafrom price ke alawa goods ko warehoufrom tak lane in incur hone wale expenses (Freight, Loading, Insurance, etc.) ko effective inventory cost in factor karna.
  • Bill of Materials (BOM): Ek finished product ko manufacture karne ke liye required raw materials and components ki predefined quantity-wifrom list (Recipe).

Golden Manufacturing Rule:
Landed Cost = Purchafrom Basic Price + Direct Acquisition Expenses.
BOM = Raw Material Components → Production Process → Finished Goods.

Practical Walkthroughs: Additional Cost & BOM Manufacturing

Part A: Additional Cost (Landed Cost Calculation)

Purchafrom Scenario: Purchased 1,000 Kg Steel Sheet @ ₹100/Kg = ₹1,00,000 from Steel Suppliers.

  • Additional Expenses: Freight = ₹5,000 | Loading = ₹2,000 | Insurance = ₹1,000 (Total = ₹8,000).
  • Total Landed Cost: ₹1,00,000 + ₹8,000 = ₹1,08,000.
  • Effective Landed Cost Per Unit: ₹1,08,000 ÷ 1,000 Kg = ₹108 / Kg.
  • TallyPrime Steps: Pass Purchafrom Voucher (F9) → Select item & rate → Allocate additional expenfrom ledgers to distribute purchafrom cost across inventory valuation.

Part B: Bill of Materials (BOM) Setup for Office Chair

BOM Structure for 1 Office Chair:

  • Wood: 3 Kg
  • Steel Legs: 5 Nos
  • Screws: 15 Nos
  • Polish: 0.25 Ltr
  • Packing Material: 1 No

Part C: Manufacturing Entry (Production of 100 Office Chairs)

  1. Go to Vouchers → Press Alt + F7 (Stock Journal / Manufacturing Journal).
  2. Select Finished Product: Office Chair → Quantity to produce: 100 Nos.
  3. Select BOM name. TallyPrime automatically computes raw material consumption:
    • Wood: 300 Kg (3 × 100)
    • Steel Legs: 500 Nos (5 × 100)
      • Screws: 1,500 Nos (15 × 100)
    • Polish: 25 Ltr (0.25 × 100)
    • Packing Material: 100 Nos (1 × 100)
  4. Save voucher → Raw material stocks decreafrom automatically while finished goods inventory increases by 100 Nos.

Verification Reports, Common Mistakes & FAQs

Verification Reports in TallyPrime:

  • Stock Summary: Verify inward purchafrom values, landed cost valuations, raw material consumption, and finished goods closing stock.
  • Stock Journal Registers: Track production batches and material issue/receipt logs.

Common Mistakes:

  • Forgetting to add freight and direct acquisition expenses to purchafrom invoices, leading to understated inventory valuation.
  • Unit mismatches between BOM components and raw material masters (e.g., Kg vs Grams).
  • Failing to link the correct BOM name during Stock Journal manufacturing entry.

Frequently Asked Questions:

    Q: Landed Cost kor is?
    A: Purchafrom price in freight, loading and insurance jefrom direct expenses add karne ke baad nikli hui effective per-unit cost.
  • Q: BOM ka real business ufrom kor hai?
    A: Finished goods produce karne ke liye required raw materials ki exact recipe maintain karna and automatic consumption track karna.
  • Q: Manufacturing entry pass karne par stock par kor impact padta hai?
    A: Raw materials ki quantity minus ho jaati hai and finished product ki quantity plus ho jaati is.
