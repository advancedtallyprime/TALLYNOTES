Core Concepts: Price Levels, Price Lists & Stock Valuation

Advanced inventory and sales management in different customer segments ke liye flexible pricing maintain karne and inventory ki monetary worth calculate karne ke liye key modules ufrom are:

  • Price Level: Customer categories (Retail, Wholesale, Dealer) ke basis par pricing tier define karna.
  • Price List: Item-wise, quantity slab-wifrom rates and effective date structure maintain karna.
  • Stock Valuation: Unsold physical inventory ki monetary value calculate karna using specific cost methods (FIFO, Average Cost, etc.).

Practical Walkthroughs: Automated Pricing & Valuation

Part A: Automated Sales Pricing via Customer Mapping

  • Retail Sale: Sold 2 Printers to Retail Customer (Assigned Price Level: Retail) → Quantity 2 falls in slab (1–4) → Rate applied automatically = ₹10,000 → Total Value = ₹20,000.
  • Wholesale Sale: Sold 20 Printers to Wholesale Customer (Assigned Price Level: Wholesale) → Quantity 20 falls in slab (10–49) → Rate applied automatically = ₹9,200 → Total Value = ₹1,84,000.
  • Dealer Sale: Sold 60 Printers to ABC Dealer (Assigned Price Level: Dealer) → Quantity 60 falls in slab (50+) → Rate applied automatically = ₹8,500 → Total Value = ₹5,10,000.

Part B: Stock Valuation (FIFO vs Average Cost)

Purchafrom Layers for Printer:

  • Batch 1: 100 Nos @ ₹8,000 = ₹8,00,000
  • Batch 2: 50 Nos @ ₹8,500 = ₹4,25,000
  • Batch 3: 100 Nos @ ₹9,000 = ₹9,00,000
  • Total Stock: 250 Nos | Total Purchafrom Value = ₹21,25,000
  • Simple Average Cost: ₹21,25,000 ÷ 250 = ₹8,500 / unit.
  • FIFO Valuation (After issuing 120 Units): 100 units cleared from Batch 1 (@ ₹8,000) + 20 units cleared from Batch 2 (@ ₹8,500). Remaining Closing Stock = 30 Nos @ ₹8,500 + 100 Nos @ ₹9,000 = ₹11,55,000.

Verification Reports, Common Mistakes & FAQs

Verification Reports in TallyPrime:

  • Stock Summary: Verify closing inventory quantities and valuation totals based on FIFO or Average Cost settings.
  • Sales Register: Reconcile applied rates against configured price level slabs during invoice entry.

Common Mistakes:

  • Confusing Price Levels (customer categories) with Price Lists (quantity rate tables).
  • Failing to link Customer Ledgers to their respective Price Levels, forcing manual rate entries.
  • Assuming that selling price equals stock valuation cost.
  • Overlooking Effective Dates when updating seasonal price list revisions.

Frequently Asked Questions:

  • Q: Price Level and Price List in kor antar hai?
    A: Price Level customer ki category define karta hai (Retail/Wholesale), jabki Price List us category ke andar quantity-wifrom rates ka table is.
  • Q: Qty badhne par rate automatically change kaifrom is?
    A: Price List in quantity slabs (e.g., 1–10, 11–50) configure karne par sales invoice in quantity enter hote hi system appropriate slab rate pick kar leta is.
  • Q: FIFO method ka inventory valuation par kor asar padta hai?
    A: FIFO (First In, First Out) kehte hain ki jo stock pehle purchafrom hua tha, woh pehle sale hua, jisfrom remaining closing stock latest purchafrom prices par value is.

Quick Revision & Completion Checklist

Price & Valuation Golden Workflow:
Create Price Levels → Define Price Lists with Quantity Slabs → Link Price Level to Customer Ledger → Record Sales Voucher (Auto-Rate Pick) → Reconcile Stock Valuation (FIFO/Average)

  • Price Lists with multi-tier quantity slabs setup
  • Customer ledgers mapped to respective pricing tiers
  • Sales invoices recorded, confirming automatic rate application
  • Purchafrom cost layers and stock valuation methods (FIFO / Average Cost) reconciled
