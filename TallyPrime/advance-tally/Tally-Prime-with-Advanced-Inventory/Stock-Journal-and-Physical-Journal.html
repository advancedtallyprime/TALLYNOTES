Core Concepts: Stock Journal vs Physical Stock Journal

Advanced inventory management in books ke andar internal stock movement ko track karne and physical warehoufrom counts ko reconcile karne ke liye do specialized journals ufrom are:

  • Stock Journal (Ctrl + F7): Internal inventory transfers (godown to godown), material consumption, production output, and scrap/wastage adjustments ke liye.
  • Physical Stock Journal: Actual physical counting ke baad system/books ki quantity ko ground reality (shortage or excess) ke sath match karne ke liye.

Comparison Matrix

Particular Stock Journal (Ctrl + F7) Physical Stock Journal
Primary Focus Internal movement, transfer & production Physical counting & reconciliation
Structure Source (Consumption/From) & Destination (Production/To) Direct entry of actual physical count per item/godown
Godown Transfer Yes (Main Godown → Branch Godown) No (Not designed for transfers)
Key Output Material consumed or position changed Shortage / Excess inventory adjustment
Golden Inventory Rule:
Stock Journal = Stock ko ek jagah from doosri jagah move karna or raw material from finish goods banana.
Physical Stock = Godown in actual count karke books ke sath reconcile karna.

Practical Walkthroughs: Godown Transfers & Reconciliation

Part A: Godown-to-Godown Transfer via Stock Journal (Ctrl + F7)

  1. Scenario: Transfer 30 Nos Printers from Main Godown to Branch Godown.
  2. TallyPrime Steps: Go to Vouchers → Press Ctrl + F7 (Stock Journal).
  3. Source (Consumption): Select Godown: Main Godown → Item: Printer → Qty: 30 Nos.
  4. Destination (Production/Receipt): Select Godown: Branch Godown → Item: Printer → Qty: 30 Nos.
  5. Save voucher → Total company stock remains unchanged (100 Nos), but location changes (Main: 70 | Branch: 30).

Part B: Physical Stock Reconciliation (Shortage / Excess)

  1. Scenario: Book stock of Printer shows 100 Nos in Main Godown, but physical physical count reveals only 96 Nos (4 Nos Shortage due to damage/breakage).
  2. TallyPrime Steps: Go to Vouchers → Select Physical Stock (or Physical Stock Journal).
  3. Select Item: Printer → Godown: Main Godown → Enter Actual Physical Quantity: 96 Nos (instead of the difference).
  4. Save voucher → Tally automatically records a 4-unit shortage adjustment. If physical count was 105, entering 105 records a 5-unit excess.

Verification Reports, Common Mistakes & FAQs

Verification Reports in TallyPrime:

  • Stock Summary / Godown Summary: Verify location-wifrom stock balances and ensure total company inventory reconciles.
  • Physical Stock Register: Review recorded shortages and excesses resulting from physical audits.

Common Mistakes:

  • Using Purchafrom or Sales vouchers for internal godown-to-godown transfers.
  • Entering the difference quantity (e.g., entering '-4') in the Physical Stock voucher instead of entering the actual physical count (e.g., '96').
  • Failing to balance Source and Destination quantities in a Stock Journal transfer, creating unaccounted inventory variance.

Frequently Asked Questions:

  • Q: Godown transfer karne from total company stock par kor asar padta hai?
    A: Pure internal transfer in total company stock same rehta hai; only storage location badalti is.
  • Q: Physical Stock voucher in kor enter karna is?
    A: System ki book quantity nahi, balki warehoufrom in count ki gayi actual physical stock quantity enter karni is.
  • Q: Stock Journal ka shortcut kor hai?
    A: Ctrl + F7.

Quick Revision & Completion Checklist

Stock Journal & Physical Stock Golden Workflow:
Stock Journal (Ctrl+F7) [Source → Destination Godown Transfer / Consumption] OR Physical Stock Voucher [Enter Actual Count → Auto-Reconcile Shortage/Excess] → Verify Godown Summary

Completion Checklist:

  • Stock Journal (Ctrl + F7) feature and godowns configured
  • Godown-to-godown stock transfers executed
  • Material consumption and production entries recorded
  • Physical stock audit conducted and shortages/excesses reconciled
  • Godown Summary and Stock Summary verified
