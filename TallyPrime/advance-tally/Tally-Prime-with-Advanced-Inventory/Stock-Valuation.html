Core Concepts: Stock Valuation, COGS & Gross Profit

Advanced inventory management in unsold closing stock ki monetary worth calculate karna financial reporting ke liye essential is:

  • Stock Valuation: Business ke paas year-end or period-end par available closing inventory ki monetary value determine karna.
  • Cost of Goods Sold (COGS): Opening Stock + Purchases − Closing Stock. Valuation method change hone from closing stock badalta hai, jisfrom COGS and final Gross Profit also alter are.

Comparison & Impact Matrix

Valuation Method Cost Flow Assumption Impact on Closing Stock in Inflation Impact on Gross Profit
FIFO (First In, First Out) Oldest costs are charged to COGS first Higher (Valued at latest/recent higher prices) Higher Profit
Average Cost Weighted average of all cost layers Moderate (Blended average valuation) Moderate Profit
Last Purchafrom Rate Valued at the most recent purchafrom cost Reflects latest market replacement cost Variable depending on market trend
Standard Rate Predefined constant standard cost Fixed standard baseline Variance-dependent
Golden Valuation Rule:
Stock Quantity batata hai ki kitne units rakhe are.
Stock Valuation batata hai ki un units ki books in cost value kitni hai (Selling price par nahi, cost price par value is).

Configuring Valuation Method in TallyPrime:

  1. Go to Gateway of Tally → Create / Alter → Stock Item (e.g., Printer).
  2. Under item configuration, locate the Method of Stock Valuation field.
  3. Select desired method: FIFO, At Average Cost, Last Purchafrom Rate, or Standard Cost.
  4. Press Ctrl + A to save.

Practical Walkthroughs: FIFO vs Average Cost Calculation

Scenario Data for Printer:

  • Opening Stock: 100 Nos @ ₹8,000 = ₹8,00,000
  • Purchafrom 1 (01-Apr): 50 Nos @ ₹8,500 = ₹4,25,000
  • Purchafrom 2 (02-May): 100 Nos @ ₹9,000 = ₹9,00,000
  • Total Available: 250 Nos | Total Purchafrom Cost = ₹21,25,000
  • Sales (31-May): 120 Nos sold @ ₹10,000 = ₹12,00,000 revenue. Closing Stock = 130 Nos.

Method 1: FIFO Valuation Calculation

    COGS (120 units issued): 100 units from Opening @ ₹8,000 (₹8,00,000) + 20 units from Purchafrom 1 @ ₹8,500 (₹1,70,000) = ₹9,70,000.
    Closing Stock (130 units left): 30 units from Purchafrom 1 @ ₹8,500 (₹2,55,000) + 100 units from Purchafrom 2 @ ₹9,000 (₹9,00,000) = ₹11,55,000.
    Gross Profit: Sales (₹12,00,000) − COGS (₹9,70,000) = ₹2,30,000.

Method 2: Average Cost Valuation Calculation

  • Weighted Average Cost per unit: Total Cost (₹21,25,000) ÷ Total Qty (250) = ₹8,500 / unit.
  • COGS (120 units issued): 120 × ₹8,500 = ₹10,20,000.
  • Closing Stock (130 units left): 130 × ₹8,500 = ₹11,05,000.
  • Gross Profit: Sales (₹12,00,000) − COGS (₹10,20,000) = ₹1,80,000.

Verification Reports, Common Mistakes & FAQs

Verification Reports in TallyPrime:

  • Stock Summary: Inspect closing stock valuation totals and per-unit rates. Drill down into item monthly summaries to verify cost layers.
  • Profit & Loss Account: Confirm how inventory valuation adjustments alter Cost of Sales and Net Profit figures.

Common Mistakes:

  • Treating selling prices as inventory acquisition costs when setting up stock item masters.
  • Assuming that changing the valuation method alters physical closing quantities (it only alters monetary valuation).
  • Ignoring opening stock layers while calculating manual FIFO or weighted averages.

Frequently Asked Questions:

  • Q: Stock valuation method change karne from physical stock quantity par kor asar padta hai?
    A: Physical quantity bilkul same rehti hai; only inventory ki monetary valuation amount change is.
  • Q: FIFO and Average Cost in kaunsa method behtar hai?
    A: Yeh company ki accounting policy and industry standards par nirbhar karta is. Rising price trends in FIFO higher profit show karta is.
  • Q: TallyPrime in valuation method kahan change is?
    A: Stock Item alteration screen in Method of Stock Valuation option ke through.

Quick Revision & Completion Checklist

Stock Valuation Golden Workflow:
Record Opening Stock & Purchases → Record Sales → Configure Valuation Method in Stock Item Master (FIFO/Average) → Review Stock Summary Valuation → Reconcile P&L Gross Profit

Completion Checklist:

  • Stock valuation concepts and financial statement impact understood
  • FIFO manual calculation and Tally output reconciled
  • Average Cost manual calculation and Tally output reconciled
  • Gross profit variance between valuation methods analyzed
  • Stock Summary and Profit & Loss reports verified
