Learning Objectives

Upon completing this session, students will be able to:

  • Actual Quantity and Billed Quantity ke beech ka fundamental difference samjhega.
  • Same transaction in physical movement (Actual Qty) and commercial charging (Billed Qty) ko separately enter karega.
  • Free/Bonus quantities (e.g., Buy 10 Get 1 Free) ka inventory and effective cost par impact samjhega.
  • Purchafrom and Sales in Actual > Billed (Excess/Free) and Actual < Billed (Short Receipt/Short Dispatch) scenarios record karega.
  • Inventory physical stock movement and invoice value calculation ke relation ko master karega.
  • Verify Purchase/Sales registers and Stock Summary from actual vs billed quantities in management and statutory tax reports.

Core Concepts: Actual Quantity vs Billed Quantity

Advanced inventory management in yeh ensure karna zaroori is ki warehoufrom in physically kitna maal aaya/gaor (Actual) and invoice par kitne units ka bill bana (Billed):

  • Actual Quantity: Warehoufrom in physically receive or dispatch hone wali exact units (Inventory ledger impact).
  • Billed Quantity: Invoice par customer or vendor dwara charge kiye jaane wali units (Financial/Billing ledger impact).

Comparison & Scenarios Matrix

Scenario Type Billed Quantity Actual Quantity Difference Meaning / Business Context
Normal Transaction 100 Nos 100 Nos No difference; standard purchafrom or sale.
Free / Bonus Qty (Actual > Billed) 100 Nos 110 Nos 10 units received free as promotional bonus. Effective unit cost decreases.
Short Receipt / Short Dispatch (Actual < Billed) 100 Nos 95 Nos 5 units short in transit or skipped during loading. Claims/adjustments required.
Golden Inventory Rule:
Actual Quantity hamesha physical stock inventory balance ko update karti is.
Billed Quantity hamesha financial invoice value and party ledger amount ko compute karti is.

Practical Walkthroughs: Free Quantity & Short Receipts

Part A: Purchafrom with Free Quantity (Actual > Billed)

  1. Scenario: Purchased 100 Printers @ ₹8,000 from Tech Supplier under a promotional scheme where supplier gives 10 Printers completely free. Total physical units received = 110.
  2. TallyPrime Steps: Go to Vouchers → Select Purchafrom (F9) → Select Party: Tech Supplier → Item: Printer.
  3. Enter Billed Qty: 100, Rate: ₹8,000, and enter Actual Qty: 110.
  4. Financial Impact: Invoice basic value is calculated on 100 units (₹8,00,000), while physical stock ledger increases by 110 units, lowering the effective purchafrom cost per unit (₹8,00,000 ÷ 110 = ₹7,272.73).

Part B: Purchafrom with Short Receipt (Actual < Billed)

  1. Scenario: Supplier invoice states 200 Moufrom @ ₹500 = ₹1,00,000, but physical verification reveals only 190 Moufrom were delivered.
  2. TallyPrime Steps: In Purchafrom Voucher, enter Billed Qty: 200 and Actual Qty: 190 at rate ₹500.
  3. Result: Party ledger gets credited for the full billed invoice value (₹1,00,000), while physical stock increases by 190 units, leaving a 10-unit discrepancy to be resolved via debit note or vendor credit.

Verification Reports, Common Mistakes & FAQs

Verification Reports in TallyPrime:

  • Purchafrom Register & Sales Register: Check column splits showing Billed Quantity vs Actual Quantity alongside invoice values.
  • Stock Summary: Confirm that inventory closing balances reflect physical Actual Quantities rather than billed line items.

Common Mistakes:

  • Entering equal actual and billed quantities out of habit, ignoring promotional free items or short shipments.
  • Calculating inventory valuation based solely on billed quantities, leading to distorted unit cost averages.
  • Confusing a short receipt (Actual < Billed) with a standard purchafrom return entry.

Frequently Asked Questions:

  • Q: Actual Quantity and Billed Quantity in mukhor antar kor hai?
    A: Actual Quantity warehoufrom in aane or jaane wale physical stock ko represent karti hai, jabki Billed Quantity invoice par charge kiye jaane wale units is.
  • Q: Free quantity milne par effective cost per unit par kor asar padta hai?
    A: Total invoice value ko jab zyaada actual units from divide kior jaata hai, toh effective acquisition cost per unit kam ho jaati is.
  • Q: Kor yeh feature sales transactions in also ufrom ho sakta hai?
    A: Haan, jab business apne customers ko bonus or promotional free units dispatch karta hai, tab Sales voucher in also yeh ufrom is.

Quick Revision & Completion Checklist

Actual vs Billed Golden Workflow:
Record Voucher (Purchase/Sales) → Enter Billed Qty (For Financial Valuation) → Enter Actual Qty (For Physical Stock) → Verify Effective Unit Cost → Reconcile Stock Summary

Completion Checklist:

  • Purchafrom entries with short receipts (Actual < Billed) recorded
  • Sales dispatch entries with promotional free items practiced
  • Purchase/Sales registers and Stock Summary reconciled
