1. Core Concept

Cheque Book Management in TallyPrime allows businesses to maintain multiple cheque books for one or more bank accounts, automate cheque numbering, prevent duplicate cheque issuance, and configure customized cheque printing coordinates for precise physical printing on bank stationery.

Key Purpose: Prevents manual cheque tracking errors, automates bank reconciliation, ensures unique serial number assignment, and saves time during bulk vendor payments.

2. Real-World Business Scenario

National Trading Co. operates a Current Account with HDFC Bank. The bank issues a 100-leaf cheque book numbered from 100001 to 100100. When issuing payments to vendors, the accountant wants TallyPrime to automatically select the next unused cheque number, display the remaining leaves, and format the printout so payee name, amount in words, and date align perfectly on the bank's pre-printed cheque leaf.

3. TallyPrime Navigation & Shortcut Workflow

Quick Navigation:
Gateway of Tally → Alter → Ledger → Select Bank Ledger (e.g. HDFC Bank A/c)
Shortcut: Alt + G → Type Alter Ledger → Select Bank

4. Step-by-Step Cheque Book Configuration

Step 1: Open Bank Ledger Alteration

  1. Navigate to Gateway of Tally → Alter → Ledger.
  2. Select the target bank account (e.g., HDFC Bank Current A/c under Bank Accounts).

Step 2: Provide Bank Details

Field Sample Configuration Purpose
A/c Holder Name National Trading Co. Official account title registered with the bank.
Account No. 50200012345678 Bank account identifier for e-payments and reporting.
IFS Code HDFC0000123 Identifies bank branch for electronic transfers.
Bank Name HDFC Bank (India) Auto-loads official bank cheque templates.
Branch MG Road Branch, Bengaluru Branch location details.

Step 3: Enable Cheque Book Ranges

  1. Under Bank Accounts Details, set Set/alter cheque books to Yes.
  2. In the Cheque Books sub-screen, enter:
    • From Number: 100001
    • To Number: 100100
    • Number of Cheques: Automatically calculated as 100
    • Cheque Book Name: HDFC Book 1 (Oct-2025)
  3. Press Ctrl + A to accept the cheque range.

Step 4: Enable Cheque Printing Configuration

  1. Set Set/alter cheque printing configuration to Yes.
  2. Select the pre-configured bank template from Tally's statutory library or customize dimensions:
    • Cheque Width & Height (in mm)
    • Distance of Payee Line from top and left edge
    • Distance of Amount in Words and Numbers
    • Signatory title and position
  3. Press Alt + P → I to Preview the sample cheque on plain paper before live printing.
  4. Press Ctrl + A to save the bank ledger.

5. Cheque Register & Status Reports

TallyPrime automatically organizes issued, available, cancelled, and post-dated cheques in the Cheque Register:

  • Path: Gateway of Tally → Banking → Cheque Register.
  • Status Codes:
    • Available: Cheque leaves in book ready to be issued.
    • Unreconciled: Cheque issued in voucher but not yet cleared in bank statement.
    • Reconciled: Cheque successfully cleared with Bank Value Date recorded.
    • Cancelled: Cheque voided due to printing mistake or clerical error (press Alt + S to mark Cancelled).
Pro Tip: Always print on a photocopy of a cheque leaf first to verify horizontal and vertical alignment before using original MICR cheque leaves!

6. Interview Questions & Answers

Q1. How do you configure multiple cheque books for a single bank in TallyPrime?

Ans: In Bank Ledger Alteration, enable Set/alter cheque books = Yes. In the table, enter separate rows with different From Number, To Number, and unique Cheque Book Names (e.g., General Vendor Book vs. Payroll Book).

Q2. What happens when all cheque numbers in an active book are consumed?

Ans: TallyPrime alerts the user that the cheque range is exhausted and prompts to select another active cheque range configured in the bank ledger.

Q3. Which shortcut opens the Cheque Printing preview directly from a Payment voucher?

Ans: When saving a Payment voucher with Bank ledger, Tally opens the Cheque Printing prompt. Press Alt + P or Ctrl + P, then I for Preview.

7. Summary & Key Takeaways

  • Cheque book configuration prevents duplicate cheque entries and maintains an automated physical register.
  • Cheque printing eliminates handwritten payment errors and enhances corporate presentation.
  • Cheque status can be monitored real-time in Banking → Cheque Register.
