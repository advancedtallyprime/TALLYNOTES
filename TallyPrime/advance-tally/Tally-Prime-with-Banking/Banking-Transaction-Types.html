Practicing BRS in TallyPrime Educational Mode:
  • You do not need a paid bank API feed to learn professional Bank Reconciliation.
  • In BRS (Alt + G → Bank Reconciliation), enter the Bank Date as 1st, 2nd, or 31st of any month to clear instruments.
  • You can also download a sample bank statement Excel/CSV file and import it via Alt + B.

1. Core Concept

Modern commercial operations conduct banking transactions through multiple channels. TallyPrime categorizes these into standardized Banking Transaction Types within payment, receipt, and contra vouchers, ensuring compliant Bank Reconciliation (BRS), electronic payment generation, and accurate audit tracking.

2. Major Banking Transaction Types in TallyPrime

Transaction Type Typical Voucher Required Details Commercial Application
Cheque / DD Payment (F5) / Receipt (F6) / Contra (F4) Cheque No., Cheque Date, Bank Name Physical bearer or crossed cheques, demand drafts for vendors/customers.
e-Fund Transfer Payment (F5) / Receipt (F6) A/c No., IFSC Code, Transfer Mode (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS), UTR / Reference No. Real-time bank transfers, online vendor disbursements, client Wire/NEFT receipts.
Electronic Cheque Payment (F5) / Receipt (F6) e-Cheque Number, Beneficiary details Digital cheques issued through institutional online banking portals.
Card (Debit / Credit) Receipt (F6) / Payment (F5) Card Number (last 4 digits), Instrument Number, Merchant ID Point-of-sale card swipe, corporate credit card expenses.
Others / Cash Deposit Contra (F4) / Receipt (F6) Denomination details, cash slip reference Cash deposited at branch counter or cash deposit machine (CDM).

3. Bank Allocation Pop-Up Screen

Whenever a bank ledger (e.g., HDFC Bank, SBI Current A/c) is debited or credited in any voucher, TallyPrime automatically displays the Bank Allocations screen.

Shortcut to View/Edit Bank Allocations: Press F12 (Configure) inside voucher entry → Ensure Provide Bank Details is set to Yes.

Key Fields in Bank Allocation Screen:

  • Transaction Type: Choose between Cheque, e-Fund Transfer, Card, Electronic Cheque, or Others.
  • Amount: Split single transaction into multiple instruments if necessary.
  • Instrument Number: Cheque number or electronic UTR reference number.
  • Instrument Date: Date printed on cheque or date transfer executed.
  • Bank Name: Drawee bank name for receipts.

4. Step-by-Step Recording: e-Fund Transfer (NEFT/RTGS)

  1. Press F5 for Payment Voucher.
  2. Debit Supplier Ledger (e.g., Apex Infotech) for ₹1,50,000.
  3. Select Against Reference (Agst Ref) for pending invoices.
  4. Credit HDFC Bank Current A/c.
  5. In the Bank Allocations window:
    • Select Transaction Type: e-Fund Transfer
    • Select Transfer Mode: NEFT or RTGS
    • Enter Instrument No. (UTR): HDFCR5202609151234
    • Confirm Beneficiary Name & IFSC.
  6. Press Ctrl + A to save the voucher.

5. Bank Reconciliation Statement (BRS)

Differences frequently arise between company book balance and the bank statement due to cheques issued but not yet presented, or deposits cleared with a delay.

BRS Navigation: Gateway of Tally → Banking → Bank Reconciliation → Select Bank Ledger.
Shortcut: Alt + G → Type Bank Reconciliation.

Auto vs Manual Reconciliation in TallyPrime:

  • Manual BRS: Review each transaction against bank statement and enter the Bank Date (value date). Once date is entered, the unreconciled balance adjusts instantly.
  • Auto BRS: Press Alt + B (Import Bank Statement). TallyPrime imports statement files (.xlsx, .csv, .xml) downloaded from net banking and matches transactions automatically by Instrument No. and Amount!

6. Interview Questions & Answers

Q1. Where do you find Bank Reconciliation in TallyPrime?

Ans: Gateway of Tally → Banking → Bank Reconciliation, or via Alt + G (Go To) → "Bank Reconciliation".

Q2. What is the difference between Instrument Date and Bank Date in BRS?

Ans: Instrument Date is the date when the cheque/transfer voucher was prepared in company books. Bank Date is the exact date when funds were debited/credited by the bank according to the bank statement.

Q3. How can multiple payment modes (e.g., part Cheque and part NEFT) be handled in one voucher?

Ans: In the Bank Allocations screen, break down the total payment into multiple rows, specifying the distinct Transaction Type, Instrument Number, and amount for each.

7. Summary & Key Takeaways

  • TallyPrime supports seamless segregation of physical cheques, cards, and electronic fund transfers (NEFT/RTGS).
  • Proper bank allocation data ensures instantaneous error-free bank reconciliations.
  • Auto BRS saves hours of reconciliation work by directly importing official bank statements.
