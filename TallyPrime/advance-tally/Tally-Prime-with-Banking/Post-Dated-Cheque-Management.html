1. Core Concept

A Post-Dated Cheque (PDC) is a cheque bearing a future settlement date. In commercial trade, buyers frequently provide post-dated cheques to suppliers to secure orders or fulfill credit terms.

TallyPrime features a specialized PDC Management Framework that enables businesses to record post-dated receipts and payments without prematurely distorting current bank balances or profit & loss statements until the maturity date arrives.

2. Why PDC Handling is Crucial

  • Accurate Cash Flow Planning: Finance managers can forecast upcoming receivables and payables based on PDC maturity schedules.
  • Audit Integrity: Prevents premature reduction of customer outstanding balances before actual cheque realization.
  • Cheque Bounce Prevention: Timely reminders ensure sufficient liquidity in current accounts before issued PDCs mature.

3. Recording a Post-Dated Voucher in TallyPrime

Magic Shortcut: Press Ctrl + T inside any Payment (F5) or Receipt (F6) voucher to toggle Post-Dated mode!
The voucher status badge at the top changes from Accounting Voucher to Post-Dated Voucher.

Step-by-Step Practical Example: Receiving a Post-Dated Cheque

Scenario: On 15th September, customer Supreme Retailers issues cheque number 445566 for ₹75,000 dated 15th October.

  1. Go to Gateway of Tally → Vouchers → Press F6 (Receipt).
  2. Press Ctrl + T to mark the voucher as Post-Dated.
  3. Press F2 (Date) and enter the Actual Cheque Date: 02-Oct-2026.
  4. In the PDC Details sub-prompt:
    • PDC Issue Date: 01-Sep-2026 (date customer physically handed over the leaf).
    • PDC Note: Security payment for Order #SR-99.
  5. Credit Supreme Retailers ₹75,000 (Against Reference of bill).
  6. Debit HDFC Bank Current A/c ₹75,000.
  7. In Bank Allocations, verify Cheque No. 445566 and Instrument Date 02-Oct-2026.
  8. Press Ctrl + A to save.
Accounting Impact: This receipt will NOT increase your bank balance on 01-Sep. It will sit in the PDC register and automatically activate into real books on 02-Oct!

4. The Post-Dated Transactions Register

Track all upcoming incoming and outgoing post-dated cheques in one centralized dashboard:

  • Path: Gateway of Tally → Banking → Post-Dated Summary.
  • Alternative: Press Alt + G → Type Post-Dated Summary.
Date Range Cheques Received (Inward) Cheques Issued (Outward) Net Projected Liquidity
October Maturity (01-Oct / 02-Oct) ₹2,50,000 ₹1,80,000 + ₹70,000 (Surplus)
November Maturity (01-Nov / 02-Nov) ₹4,00,000 ₹3,50,000 + ₹50,000 (Surplus)
December Maturity (31-Dec) ₹1,20,000 ₹2,90,000 - ₹1,70,000 (Deficit - Arrange OD)
Educational Mode Practice Tip:

In TallyPrime Educational Mode, always enter post-dated cheques with maturity dates set to the 1st, 2nd, or 31st of the upcoming month (e.g. 01-Nov-2025 or 02-Dec-2025). Tally will smoothly hold and track them in the Post-Dated Summary without date rejection errors.

5. Converting Post-Dated Cheque to Regular Voucher

When the maturity date arrives and the cheque is deposited in the bank:

  1. Open Post-Dated Summary.
  2. Select the mature voucher.
  3. Press Ctrl + T again to toggle off Post-Dated status, or let Tally automatically realize it based on the voucher date.
  4. Perform BRS and enter the clearance date!

6. Interview Questions & Answers

Q1. Which shortcut key is used in TallyPrime to mark a voucher as Post-Dated?

Ans: Ctrl + T toggles between Regular and Post-Dated voucher status.

Q2. Do post-dated receipts affect the current Balance Sheet on the day they are received?

Ans: No. Post-dated vouchers do not affect regular financial statements until their effective instrument date is reached.

Q3. Where can management check the list of all pending post-dated cheques?

Ans: Under Gateway of Tally → Banking → Post-Dated Summary.

7. Summary & Key Takeaways

  • Use Ctrl + T to record forward-dated cheques without affecting current balances.
  • Post-Dated Summary provides cash flow predictability to treasury managers.
  • PDCs automatically transition into regular accounting records upon maturity.
