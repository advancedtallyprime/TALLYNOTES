1. Topic Overview & Reference Summary

This reference note summarizes the complete lifecycle of Post-Dated Cheques (PDC) in TallyPrime, providing a quick revision guide, common field checklist, and operational best practices for accounting professionals.

Practical Demonstration Focus: Comprehensive walkthrough of PDC issuance to suppliers, PDC collection from debtors, discounting PDCs with banks, and handling dishonored/bounced post-dated cheques.

2. Complete PDC Lifecycle Workflow

Stage Action in TallyPrime Shortcut / Menu Path Accounting Result
1. Receipt of PDC Create Receipt Voucher (F6), activate Post-Dated with Ctrl + T. Enter future date. F6 → Ctrl + T → F2 (Due Date) Recorded in PDC Register only; current bank balance unchanged.
2. Custody & Tracking Monitor maturity calendar in Post-Dated Summary. Gateway of Tally → Banking → Post-Dated Summary Upcoming cash inflows projected accurately.
3. Bank Deposit on Maturity Deposit physical cheque at bank counter; voucher matures into active accounts. Regularizes on voucher maturity date Customer ledger credited; Bank account debited.
4. Bank Clearance (BRS) Reconcile instrument in Bank Reconciliation statement with Bank Date. Banking → Bank Reconciliation Cleared from BRS unreconciled pool.
5. Exception: Cheque Return / Bounce Pass reverse Journal / Payment entry charging bank bounce penalty to customer. Vouchers → F7 (Journal) Customer debtor balance restored + bank charges debited to customer.

3. Key Shortcut Cheat Sheet for Banking

  • Ctrl + T — Mark Voucher as Post-Dated / Regular
  • Alt + G — Instant Go-To Navigation (Banking, BRS, Cheque Register)
  • F5 — Payment Voucher (Vendor Disbursements)
  • F6 — Receipt Voucher (Customer Collections)
  • F4 — Contra Voucher (Cash Deposit / Withdrawal / Bank Transfer)
  • Alt + B — Import Electronic Bank Statement in BRS

4. Handling Cheque Bounce Scenario in TallyPrime

If customer cheque of ₹50,000 bounces due to insufficient funds, and the bank levies ₹590 penalty charges:

  1. Open Journal Voucher (F7).
  2. Debit Customer Ledger with ₹50,590 (original amount + bounce charges).
  3. Credit Bank Ledger with ₹50,000 (reversing the credited deposit).
  4. Credit Bank Charges Recovery / Income Ledger with ₹590 (recovering fee from customer).
  5. Provide Cheque Return Memo reference in narration.

5. Interview Questions & Answers

Q1. Why should post-dated cheques never be recorded as regular current-dated receipts?

Ans: Recording a future cheque as current receipt falsely inflates the company's ledger bank balance and prematurely extinguishes the customer's legal debt obligation before funds are guaranteed.

Q2. How do you account for bank charges incurred when a customer's cheque bounces?

Ans: Pass a Journal (F7) debiting the customer's ledger for the bounced cheque amount plus bank charges, and crediting the bank account.

6. Summary & Key Takeaways

  • PDC management safeguards financial accuracy and gives full visibility into future liquidity.
  • Combined with Bank Reconciliation, TallyPrime delivers enterprise-grade banking control.
