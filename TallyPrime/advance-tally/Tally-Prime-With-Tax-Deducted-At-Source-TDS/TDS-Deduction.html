1. Advanced Scenarios in TDS Deduction

This note explores complex commercial deduction workflows: deduction on advance payments, multi-rate split contracts, and managing threshold limit crossings mid-year.

2. TDS on Advance Payments

If an advance payment of ₹2,00,000 is made to a contractor before receiving their service invoice:

  1. Open Payment Voucher (F5).
  2. Debit Contractor Ledger ₹2,00,000 (Reference Type = Advance).
  3. Credit TDS on Contract A/c (194C) ₹4,000 (2% on ₹2,00,000).
  4. Credit Bank A/c ₹1,96,000.
  5. When the final bill arrives later, select Agst Ref to adjust the advance and deduct TDS only on the incremental unpaid balance!

3. Mid-Year Threshold Exemption Crossing

When multiple smaller payments to a vendor cumulatively cross the statutory threshold during the financial year:

  • Payment 1: ₹10,000 (Below 194C single threshold → No TDS deducted).
  • Payment 2: ₹15,000 (Cumulative ₹25,000 → No TDS).
  • Payment 3: ₹80,000 (Cumulative reaches ₹1,05,000, exceeding ₹1,00,000 annual threshold!).
  • TallyPrime Action: Tally instantly flags the cumulative crossing and calculates TDS not just on ₹80,000, but retroactively on the entire ₹1,05,000!

4. Interview Questions & Answers

Q1. What happens when a vendor's total transactions exceed the annual threshold limit mid-year?

Ans: TallyPrime automatically brings earlier untaxed amounts into the tax bracket and computes TDS on the full cumulative value in the voucher that breaches the threshold.
