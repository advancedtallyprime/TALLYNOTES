1. Core Concept

TDS entries are typically recorded in Purchase Voucher (F9) (Accounting Invoice mode) or Journal Voucher (F7) when booking services rendered by contractors, professionals, or landlords.

2. Practical Transaction Scenario

On 10th October 2025, company receives a professional consulting bill of ₹1,00,000 from Dr. Sharma & Associates (PAN: AASPS1234F). Applicable TDS under Section 194J is 10% (₹10,000). Net amount payable to consultant is ₹90,000.

3. Step-by-Step Booking via Journal Voucher (F7)

  1. Go to Gateway of Tally → Vouchers → Press F7 (Journal).
  2. Press F2 and set date to 01-Oct-2025.
  3. Debit: Legal & Professional Fees A/c → ₹1,00,000.
    • Select TDS Nature of Payment: Fees for Professional or Technical Services.
  4. Credit: Dr. Sharma & Associates → Tally auto-computes:
    • Bill Amount: ₹1,00,000
    • TDS Deducted: ₹10,000
    • Net Credited to Party: ₹90,000!
  5. In Bill-wise details: select New Ref for bill INV-889.
  6. Credit: TDS on Professional Fees A/c → ₹10,000.
    • Tally auto-populates the TDS Details screen linked to Party PAN and Section 194J!
  7. Press Ctrl + A to save.
Accounting Equation Verified:
Debit: Professional Expense (₹1,00,000)
Credit: Creditor Party (₹90,000) + Credit: TDS Payable (₹10,000) = ₹1,00,000!

4. Interview Questions & Answers

Q1. Can TDS on expenses be booked in a Purchase Voucher instead of Journal?

Ans: Yes. By switching the Purchase Voucher (F9) to Accounting Invoice mode (Ctrl + H), professional services, rent, and contractor bills can be booked with vendor GST and TDS computed simultaneously.
