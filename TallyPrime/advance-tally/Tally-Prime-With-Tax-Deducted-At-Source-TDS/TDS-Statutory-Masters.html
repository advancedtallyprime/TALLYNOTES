1. Core Concept: Nature of Payment

In TallyPrime, TDS calculation rules are governed by statutory masters termed TDS Nature of Payment. Each Nature of Payment corresponds to an official section in the Income Tax Act, storing section code, threshold limits, and applicable tax rates.

2. Creating a TDS Nature of Payment

Navigation: Gateway of Tally → Create → TDS Nature of Payments.
Field Configuration Example (Rent) Configuration Example (Professional Fees)
Name Rent of Land, Building or Furniture Fees for Professional or Technical Services
Section 194I 194J
Payment Code 94I 94J
Rate for Individuals/HUF (with PAN) 10% 10%
Rate for Other Deductee Types 10% 10%
Threshold / Exemption Limit ₹2,40,000 ₹30,000
Auto-Helper: In TallyPrime, when creating a Nature of Payment, press Alt + H (Helper) to view and select from the pre-defined statutory list updated by Tally Solutions!

3. Interview Questions & Answers

Q1. What shortcut helper lists predefined TDS Natures of Payment in TallyPrime?

Ans: Press Alt + H (Helper) inside the TDS Nature of Payment creation screen.
