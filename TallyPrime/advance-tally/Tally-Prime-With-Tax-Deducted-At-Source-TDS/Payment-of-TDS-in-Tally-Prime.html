1. Core Concept of Government Tax Remittance

Deducted TDS must be deposited into authorized banks (like SBI, HDFC, ICICI) to the credit of the Central Government using Challan ITNS 281 on or before the 7th of each month.

2. Automated Statutory Payment Workflow (Ctrl + F)

TallyPrime includes an automated Statutory Payment (Auto-Fill) tool that eliminates manual voucher calculations and ensures exact challan generation.

Shortcut: In Payment Voucher (F5), press Ctrl + F (Auto Fill) → Select Stat TDS Payment!

Step-by-Step Deposit Walkthrough:

  1. Go to Vouchers → Press F5 (Payment).
  2. Press Ctrl + F (Autofill) → Select Stat Payment.
  3. In Statutory Payment prompt:
    • Tax Type: TDS
    • Period From & To: 01-Oct-2025 to 31-Oct-2025
    • Deducted Till Date: 31-Oct-2025
    • Section: 194J (or All Sections)
    • Nature of Payment: Fees for Professional or Technical Services
    • Deductee Status: Non-Company (or All)
    • Bank Ledger: HDFC Bank Current A/c
  4. Tally automatically fetches all pending TDS deductions for October and populates the debit and credit rows!
  5. In Bank Allocation, enter e-Tax transfer reference.
  6. In Challan Details sub-screen, enter:
    • BSR Code: 7-digit branch code (e.g. 0510304)
    • Challan No.: Serial number generated on tax portal (e.g. 10092)
    • Challan Date: Date tax deposited
  7. Press Ctrl + A to save.

3. Printing Official Challan ITNS 281

Open the payment voucher → Press Ctrl + P → Tally prints the official Challan ITNS 281 complete with TAN, Major Head (0020/0021), Minor Head (200), Assessment Year, and tax break-up.

4. Interview Questions & Answers

Q1. Which shortcut key triggers the Statutory Payment Autofill in TallyPrime?

Ans: Ctrl + F (Autofill) inside the Payment voucher.

Q2. What are the two Major Heads on Challan 281?

Ans: 0020 for Company Deductees (Corporation Tax) and 0021 for Non-Company Deductees (Income Tax other than companies).
