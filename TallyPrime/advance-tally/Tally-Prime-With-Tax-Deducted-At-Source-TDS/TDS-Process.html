1. Core Concept of the 4-Stage TDS Lifecycle

Managing TDS in an enterprise involves a cyclical 4-stage compliance journey. TallyPrime automates each stage, eliminating arithmetic mistakes and regulatory penalties.

Stage Key Compliance Action Statutory Due Date Form / Challan Involved
Stage 1: Deduction Deduct tax while booking expense invoice (F9/F7) or making advance payment. Date of invoice booking or payment (whichever earlier) Journal / Purchase Voucher
Stage 2: Government Deposit Deposit deducted tax to Central Government account via authorized banks. 7th of the following month (30th April for March deductions) Challan ITNS 281
Stage 3: Quarterly e-TDS Return File electronic TDS statement detailing deductee PANs, payments, and challan BSR numbers. 31st of the month following the quarter (31-Jul, 31-Oct, 31-Jan, 31-May) Form 24Q (Salary) / Form 26Q (Non-Salary)
Stage 4: Certificate Issuance Download digitally signed TDS certificate from TRACES and furnish to deductee. Within 15 days from due date of return filing Form 16 (Salary) / Form 16A (Non-Salary)

2. Consequences of Non-Compliance

  • Interest for Non-Deduction: 1% per month or part of a month from the date tax was deductible until deducted.
  • Interest for Late Deposit: 1.5% per month or part of a month from date of deduction until actual deposit date.
  • Disallowance of Expense (Section 40(a)(ia)): 30% of the expenditure is disallowed for computing taxable business profit if TDS is not deposited!
  • Late Filing Fee (Section 234E): ₹200 per day until the quarterly return is filed.

3. Interview Questions & Answers

Q1. What is the due date for depositing TDS deducted in the month of October?

Ans: 7th November. (TDS must always be deposited by the 7th of the following calendar month, except March which has a deadline of 30th April).
