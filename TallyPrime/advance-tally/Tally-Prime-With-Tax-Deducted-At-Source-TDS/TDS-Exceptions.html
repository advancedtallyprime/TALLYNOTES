1. Core Concept of TDS Exceptions

In statutory tax administration, several scenarios deviate from standard flat-rate deduction rules. TallyPrime features automated exception rules for invalid/absent PANs, lower deduction certificates, and zero deduction declarations.

2. Primary TDS Exceptions in TallyPrime

Exception Type Governing Section Rule & Mechanics How Configured in Tally
Deduction at Higher Rate (No PAN) Section 206AA & 206AB If deductee fails to provide PAN or is a non-filer, tax MUST be deducted at 20% (or twice standard rate, whichever higher). Leave PAN field blank in Party Ledger; Tally auto-applies 20% on invoice.
Lower Deduction Certificate Section 197 Assessee obtains official certificate from Assessing Officer authorizing deduction at lower rate (e.g., 2% instead of 10%). In Party Master → Use Advanced TDS Entries = Yes → Enter Certificate No. & Valid Period.
Zero Deduction (Form 15G / 15H) Section 197A Resident individuals submit self-declaration declaring estimated annual income is below taxable threshold. In Party Master → Set Zero Rate deduction under Section 197A.

3. Interview Questions & Answers

Q1. What is the statutory TDS rate under Section 206AA if a supplier fails to furnish their PAN?

Ans: 20% flat rate (or standard rate, whichever is higher).

Q2. How is a Section 197 Lower Deduction Certificate tracked in TallyPrime?

Ans: Enable Use Advanced TDS Entries in Party Ledger, enter the Certificate Number issued by Income Tax Dept, specify the validity date range, and assign the sanctioned lower tax rate.
