1. Prominent TDS Sections & Threshold Limits

Under the Indian Income Tax framework, TDS applies only when payments exceed statutory Threshold Limits specified under respective sections of the Income Tax Act.

Section Nature of Payment Threshold Limit Standard TDS Rate
194C Payments to Contractors / Subcontractors Single bill > ₹30,000 or Aggregate > ₹1,00,000 / year 1% (Individual/HUF), 2% (Other entities)
194J Fees for Professional or Technical Services ₹30,000 per financial year 10% (Professional), 2% (Technical services)
194I Rent on Land, Building, or Furniture ₹2,40,000 per financial year 10% (Land/Building), 2% (Plant & Machinery)
194H Commission or Brokerage ₹15,000 per financial year 5%
194A Interest other than Interest on Securities (Bank FD / Loans) ₹40,000 (₹50,000 for Senior Citizens) 10%

2. When Should TDS be Deducted?

According to Income Tax law, TDS must be deducted at the earliest of the following two events:

  • At the time of crediting the amount to the account of the payee (booking the expense invoice in books).
  • At the time of actual payment in cash, cheque, or electronic transfer (advance payment).

3. Interview Questions & Answers

Q1. What is the TDS rate under Section 194J for Professional Services?

Ans: 10% for professional services (e.g., Legal, CA, Architectural fees) and 2% for technical services / call center fees.

Q2. If a company pays monthly rent of ₹15,000 to a landlord, is TDS deductible under Section 194I?

Ans: Total annual rent = ₹15,000 × 12 = ₹1,80,000. Since this is below the statutory threshold of ₹2,40,000 per annum, NO TDS is deductible.
