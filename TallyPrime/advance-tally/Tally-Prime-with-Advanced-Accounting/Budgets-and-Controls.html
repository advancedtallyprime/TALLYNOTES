1. Core Concept

  • Budgets: Financial targets set for expenses, incomes, or ledgers over a specific timeframe (e.g., monthly, quarterly, annually) to manage cash flow and operational costs.
  • Variance Analysis: The process of comparing your actual expenses and incomes against your pre-set budget figures to identify overspending, cost savings, or revenue shortfalls.
  • Controls: Management tools in TallyPrime that alert you or provide reporting insights when expenses exceed budgeted limits.
  • Budget Allocation Types in TallyPrime:
    • On Net Transactions: Compares the budget against total net changes (debits minus credits) during the given period. (Best for expense and income accounts).
    • On Closing Balance: Compares the budget against the cumulative closing balance at the end of the period. (Best for assets and liability accounts).

2. Enabling & Creating Budgets in TallyPrime

Step A: Enable Budget Feature

  1. Press F11 (Features) on the Gateway of Tally.
  2. Set Enable Maintain Budgets and Controls to Yes.
  3. Press Ctrl + A to save.

Step B: Create a Budget Master

  1. Gateway of Tally → Create → Budget (or press Alt + G → Create Master → Budget).
  2. Enter the Name of the budget (e.g., Marketing Budget FY 26-27 or Q1 Expense Budget).
  3. Set the Period of Budget:
    • From: 1-Apr-2026
    • To: 31-Jul-2026 (Quarter 1)
  4. Set Budget Parameters for Group or Ledger:
    • Set/Alter Groups: Set to Yes if assigning a single budget limit to an entire group (e.g., Indirect Expenses).
    • Set/Alter Ledgers: Set to Yes if setting specific limits for individual ledgers (e.g., Advertising, Rent, Travel Expenses).
  5. Specify the allocation type (On Net Transactions or On Closing Balance) and enter the budget amounts.
  6. Press Ctrl + A to save.

5. Report Verification & Variance Analysis

How to View Budget Variance Reports in TallyPrime:

  • Gateway of Tally → Display More Reports → Statement of Accounts → Budgets.
  • Alternative Shortcut: Press Alt + G (Go To) → Type Budget Variance.
  • Select your budget name (e.g., Q1 Marketing & Admin Budget).
  • Press F10 (Budget Variance) to toggle variance view on/off in the Profit & Loss or Trial Balance screen.

Interpreting Columns:

  • Target Column: Shows the budgeted target figures.
  • Actual Column: Shows actual voucher postings during the period.
  • Variance Column: Shows the difference in amount and percentage (+ values indicate savings, - values indicate overspending).
