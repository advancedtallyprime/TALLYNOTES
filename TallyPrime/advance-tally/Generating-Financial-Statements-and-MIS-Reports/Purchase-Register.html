1. Core Concept

The Purchase Register provides a comprehensive month-by-month and invoice-by-invoice audit trail of all goods and services procured by the enterprise. It plays an indispensable role in tracking Input Tax Credit (ITC), supplier invoice reconciliation, and inventory valuation.

2. Accessing & Customizing Purchase Register

Navigation: Gateway of Tally → Display More Reports → Account Books → Purchase Register.
Shortcut: Alt + G → Type Purchase Register.

3. Advanced Columnar View in Purchase Register

By pressing F8 (Columnar) inside the Purchase Register, users can extract a multi-column procurement report:

Date Supplier Invoice No. Party Name Supplier GSTIN Gross Value CGST SGST IGST Total Bill Amount
02-Oct-2025 INV-9021 Apex Distributors 29AABCA1234F1Z5 ₹1,00,000 ₹9,000 ₹9,000 - ₹1,18,000
01-Oct-2025 DL-7782 Global Tech Importers 27AABCG8899K1Z2 ₹2,50,000 - - ₹45,000 ₹2,95,000

4. Reconciling with GSTR-2B

  1. Export Purchase Register to Excel via Alt + E.
  2. Download auto-drafted GSTR-2B from the GST Portal.
  3. Match Supplier GSTIN, Invoice Numbers, and Tax amounts to ensure eligible Input Tax Credit (ITC) is claimed without risk of notice under Section 16(2)(aa).

5. Interview Questions & Answers

Q1. Which shortcut key enables the Columnar format in the Purchase Register?

Ans: F8 (Columnar) activates the multi-column register displaying separate tax, freight, and discount columns.
