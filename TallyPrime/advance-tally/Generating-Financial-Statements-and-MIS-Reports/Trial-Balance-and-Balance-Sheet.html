1. Core Concept

Trial Balance and Balance Sheet are the twin cornerstones of financial reporting. The Trial Balance proves mathematical accuracy of double-entry ledger postings, while the Balance Sheet reveals the financial net worth (Assets = Liabilities + Capital) of an enterprise at a specific cut-off date.

2. Trial Balance in TallyPrime

Path: Gateway of Tally → Display More Reports → Trial Balance (Alt + G → "Trial Balance").

Key Features of Trial Balance:

  • Mathematical Proof: Total Debit balance must always equal Total Credit balance.
  • Drill-Down Capability: Press Enter on any Group (e.g. Current Assets) → reveals Subgroups → reveals individual Ledgers → reveals Monthly Summary → reveals original Vouchers!
  • Detailed View (Alt + F5): Expands all sub-groups to display individual ledger closing balances.

3. Balance Sheet in TallyPrime

Path: Gateway of Tally → Balance Sheet (or press B from Gateway).

Horizontal vs. Vertical Balance Sheet Formats:

Format Type Layout Structure Standard Usage How to Switch in Tally
Horizontal (T-Format) Liabilities & Capital on Left; Assets on Right. Proprietorships, Partnerships, Indian traditional accounting. Default view in TallyPrime.
Vertical (Schedule III) Sources of Funds (Equity & Liabilities) on Top; Application of Funds (Assets) at Bottom. Corporate Companies under Companies Act 2013 / Ind-AS. Press F12 → Set Show Vertical Balance Sheet = Yes.

4. Step-by-Step Year-End Balance Sheet Finalization

  1. Verify that the Difference in Opening Balances in Trial Balance is ZERO.
  2. Pass closing provisions (Depreciation, Prepaid Expenses, Accrued Incomes, Outstanding Salaries) via Journal (F7).
  3. Reconcile Bank Accounts with bank statements and clear all BRS discrepancies.
  4. Check that Negative Cash or Negative Stock is completely resolved.
  5. Review Closing Stock value calculated under Selected Stock Valuation Method (FIFO / Average Cost).
  6. Export finalized Balance Sheet to PDF/Excel via Alt + E.

5. Interview Questions & Answers

Q1. If the Debit and Credit of a Trial Balance match, does it guarantee zero accounting errors?

Ans: No. Matching totals only prove arithmetic equality. Errors of principle (e.g., treating capital expenditure as revenue expense) or errors of omission do not disturb Trial Balance equality.

Q2. How can you view a comparative Balance Sheet of two financial years in TallyPrime?

Ans: Open Balance Sheet → Press Alt + C (New Column) → Enter the date range of the previous financial year.
