1. Core Concept

The Profit & Loss Account (P&L) measures the operational performance and profitability of a business over a specified period. It calculates Gross Profit from trading activities (Sales vs. Direct Costs) and deducts operating/non-operating overheads to derive Net Profit.

2. Two-Stage Structure of P&L Account

Section Components Result / Key Metric
Trading Account (Upper Half) Debit: Opening Stock, Purchases, Direct Expenses (Freight, Factory Wages, Power).
Credit: Sales Accounts, Closing Stock. 		Gross Profit (if Sales > Costs) or Gross Loss.
Profit & Loss Account (Lower Half) Debit: Indirect Expenses (Rent, Salaries, Electricity, Depreciation, Interest Paid, Marketing).
Credit: Gross Profit b/d, Indirect Incomes (Interest Received, Discount Received, Commission). 		Net Profit (transferred to Capital Account in Balance Sheet).

3. Navigating & Customizing P&L in TallyPrime

Access Path: Gateway of Tally → Profit & Loss A/c (or press P from Gateway).

Valuable F12 Configurations in P&L:

  • Show as Percentage: Press F12 → Set Show as Percentage = Yes. Tally computes every expense as a percentage of Total Sales, highlighting cost drivers!
  • Schedule III Format: Set Show Vertical Profit & Loss = Yes for corporate compliance format.
  • Detailed View: Press Alt + F5 to see ledger-by-ledger expense breakdowns.

4. Interview Questions & Answers

Q1. What is the fundamental difference between Direct Expenses and Indirect Expenses in TallyPrime?

Ans: Direct Expenses (wages, carriage inward, factory power) directly relate to production/procurement and impact Gross Profit. Indirect Expenses (office rent, administrative salaries, marketing) relate to general operations and impact Net Profit.

Q2. How is Closing Stock valued in the Profit & Loss Account in TallyPrime?

Ans: Tally automatically calculates Closing Stock value based on inventory transactions and the assigned stock valuation method (e.g. Average Cost, FIFO).
