1. Core Concept

TallyPrime delivers a next-generation reporting engine engineered around flexibility, speed, and real-time computation. With the powerful Go To (Alt + G) search bar, multi-tasking navigation, and the Save View feature, users can generate, customize, and preserve executive MIS views instantly.

2. Key MIS Pillars in TallyPrime

Report Category Key Reports Included Access Shortcut
Financial Statements Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, Trial Balance, Cash Flow, Funds Flow Alt + G → Type "Balance Sheet" / "P&L"
Accounting & Working Capital Bills Receivable, Bills Payable, Ageing Analysis, Cash/Bank Summary Alt + G → Type "Receivables"
Inventory MIS Stock Summary, Reorder Status, Movement Analysis, Batch Expiry Alt + G → Type "Stock Summary"
Exception & Audit MIS Negative Stock, Negative Ledgers, Overdue Receivables, Cancelled Vouchers Alt + G → Type "Exception Reports"

3. Essential Report Modification Keys in TallyPrime

Universal Report Shortcuts:
  • F12 — Configure current report layout, columns, percentage display, and sorting.
  • Alt + F12 (Range / Filter) — Filter transactions matching custom conditions (e.g., bills > ₹50,000).
  • Alt + F5 / Alt + F1 — Toggle Detailed vs. Condensed view.
  • Alt + C — Add new comparative column (compare Q1 vs Q2 or Year 2025 vs 2026).
  • Ctrl + L — Save View: Save custom filtered view as your personalized report!

4. Multi-Tasking with "Go To" (Alt + G)

One of TallyPrime's greatest innovations is seamless multi-tasking:

  1. While entering a large 50-item Sales Voucher, customer requests their outstanding balance.
  2. Instead of abandoning the voucher, press Alt + G → Type Bills Outstanding.
  3. Inspect the customer's ledger, answer their inquiry.
  4. Press Esc — Tally brings you right back to your half-entered voucher with zero lost data!

5. Interview Questions & Answers

Q1. How does the "Save View" feature in TallyPrime benefit finance managers?

Ans: It allows users to save custom configurations (like filtered debtors with overdue > 30 days) under a custom name, accessible immediately without reconfiguring every time.

Q2. What is an Exception Report in TallyPrime?

Ans: Reports highlighting business anomalies such as Negative Cash balance, Negative Stock quantities, or overdue bills requiring urgent managerial intervention.
