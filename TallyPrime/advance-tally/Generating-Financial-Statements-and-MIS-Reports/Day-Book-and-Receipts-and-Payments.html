1. Core Concept

The Day Book is the chronological chronological master journal of all transactions recorded on a given day or period. The Receipts and Payments Account is a classified summary of all cash and bank transactions, serving as the liquid register of an organization.

2. Working with the Day Book

Navigation: Gateway of Tally → Day Book (or press K from Gateway).

Key Function Keys in Day Book:

  • F2 (Period): Change date to view transactions for a specific day, or press Alt + F2 to view a date range (e.g. 1-Apr to 31-May).
  • F4 (Voucher Type): Filter Day Book to show only Sales, Purchases, Receipts, or Payments.
  • Alt + F12 (Filter): Search for entries by narration text, specific voucher numbers, or amount thresholds.
  • Alt + X (Cancel Voucher): Cancels a voucher while preserving its sequential voucher number for audit compliance.
  • Alt + D (Delete Voucher): Permanently deletes selected voucher.

3. Receipts and Payments Summary

Path: Gateway of Tally → Display More Reports → Receipts and Payments.
  • Lists all opening Cash & Bank balances on the debit side.
  • Summarizes all receipts during the period under Capital vs. Revenue heads.
  • Summarizes all payments during the period.
  • Concludes with the closing Cash & Bank balances.

4. Interview Questions & Answers

Q1. What is the difference between cancelling a voucher (Alt + X) and deleting a voucher (Alt + D) in Day Book?

Ans: Cancelling keeps the voucher number intact, marks the status as "Cancelled", and sets amounts to zero, preventing numbering gaps for statutory audits. Deleting permanently erases the voucher, which can cause numbering gaps.
