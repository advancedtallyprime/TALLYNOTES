1. Core Concept

Bills Receivable represents pending invoice payments due from customers (Sundry Debtors), while Bills Payable represents outstanding obligations due to suppliers (Sundry Creditors). Managing these outstandings is essential for maintaining liquidity and minimizing bad debts.

2. Outstandings Navigation in TallyPrime

Fast Navigation: Gateway of Tally → Display More Reports → Statements of Accounts → Outstandings.
Shortcuts:
  • Alt + G → Type Bills Receivable
  • Alt + G → Type Bills Payable

3. Ageing Analysis (Overdue Slab Tracking)

Not all pending bills carry equal risk. An invoice overdue by 5 days is normal; an invoice overdue by 120 days requires aggressive recovery action.

Metro Retail Hub ₹3,50,000 - ₹1,00,000 ₹1,50,000 ₹1,00,000

How to Configure Ageing Analysis in Tally:

  1. Open Bills Receivable report.
  2. Press F6 (Age-wise).
  3. Choose Ageing by Due Date or Ageing by Bill Date.
  4. Define age slabs: 0 to 30, 30 to 60, 60 to 90, > 90 days.

4. Sending Automated Reminder Letters

  1. In Bills Outstanding, select target debtor.
  2. Press Ctrl + P (Print) → Select Reminder Letter.
  3. Tally generates a formal payment reminder letter with company header, outstanding bill references, overdue interest clauses, and bank remittance details ready to email or print.

5. Interview Questions & Answers

Q1. Which key is used to settle multiple outstanding bills simultaneously in TallyPrime?

Ans: In Outstandings report, select bills using Spacebar, then press Alt + B (Settle Bills). Tally automatically creates a pre-filled Receipt or Payment voucher.

Q2. What is the difference between Ageing by Bill Date vs. Ageing by Due Date?

Ans: Ageing by Bill Date calculates overdue days starting from the original invoice date. Ageing by Due Date starts counting overdue days only after the allowed credit period (e.g. 30 days credit) expires.
