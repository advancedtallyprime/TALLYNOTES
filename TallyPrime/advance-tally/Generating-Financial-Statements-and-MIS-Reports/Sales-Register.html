1. Core Concept

The Sales Register is the authoritative journal recording all revenue-generating outward supplies. It provides management with vital data on product demand, customer purchasing trends, regional sales distribution, and GST output liability.

2. Navigating the Sales Register in TallyPrime

Navigation: Gateway of Tally → Display More Reports → Account Books → Sales Register.
Shortcut: Alt + G → Type Sales Register.

3. Analytical Capabilities of Sales Register

  • Periodic Trends: Monthly bar graphs illustrate revenue expansion or seasonal dips.
  • Party-Wise Breakup: Press Enter → Press F4 or Ctrl + H (Change View) to group sales by Customer, Voucher Type, or Inventory Item.
  • Columnar Details (F8): Generates columns for Basic Value, Item Quantities, Cash/Trade Discounts, Freight Charges, and GST Breakdown.
  • Quarterly Comparison (Alt + C): Compare Q1 vs. Q2 sales performance across customer segments.

4. Exporting Sales Data for e-Invoicing & GSTR-1

TallyPrime directly integrates Sales Register entries with statutory filings:

  1. Press Alt + E → GST Returns → GSTR-1.
  2. Tally organizes sales invoices into Table 4A (B2B Invoices), Table 5A (B2C Large), Table 7 (B2C Small), and Table 9B (Credit/Debit Notes).
  3. Generate e-Invoice JSON files with QR Code and IRN directly from sales records.

5. Interview Questions & Answers

Q1. How can you identify your top 5 revenue-generating customers from the Sales Register?

Ans: Change View to Party-wise summary, sort columns by total turnover in descending order via F12 configurations.
