1. Core Concept

Ratio Analysis is a powerful financial diagnostic tool that evaluates the financial health, liquidity, operational efficiency, and profitability of a company by calculating mathematical relationships between key figures in the Balance Sheet and P&L Account.

2. Key Financial Ratios in TallyPrime

Financial Ratio Formula in TallyPrime Benchmark Norm Significance
Current Ratio Total Current Assets ÷ Total Current Liabilities 2 : 1 Measures short-term debt-paying solvency.
Quick / Acid Test Ratio (Current Assets − Closing Stock) ÷ Current Liabilities 1 : 1 Tests immediate liquidity without relying on inventory liquidation.
Debt-Equity Ratio Total Loans & Liabilities ÷ Net Worth (Capital + Reserves) ≤ 2 : 1 Indicates financial leverage and solvency risk.
Gross Profit % (Gross Profit ÷ Net Sales) × 100 Industry specific (> 20-30%) Core production and trading efficiency.
Net Profit % (Net Profit ÷ Net Sales) × 100 Industry specific (> 10-15%) Overall operational and managerial profitability.
Stock Turnover Ratio Cost of Goods Sold ÷ Average Stock Higher is better Speed at which inventory is converted into sales.

3. Accessing Ratio Analysis in TallyPrime

Navigation: Gateway of Tally → Ratio Analysis (or press R from Gateway).

The screen divides neatly into two sections: Principal Groups (Capital, Net Worth, Current Assets, Current Liabilities) on the left, and Calculated Principal Ratios on the right.

4. Interview Questions & Answers

Q1. What does a Current Ratio of 0.8 : 1 indicate in TallyPrime?

Ans: It signals severe short-term liquidity risk; the business possesses only 80 paise of liquid assets for every ₹1 of short-term debts due.

Q2. How can management improve a poor Stock Turnover Ratio?

Ans: Liquidate obsolete/slow-moving inventory, optimize reorder levels, and negotiate smaller frequent replenishment batches with suppliers.
