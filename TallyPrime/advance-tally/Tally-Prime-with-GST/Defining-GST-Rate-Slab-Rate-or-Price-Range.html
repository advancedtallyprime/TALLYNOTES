Core Concept: GST Classification & Rate Slabs

GST Classification: Similar GST characteristics wale transactions or items ko centrally define karne ka mechanism hai taaki har item in manually rate configure na karna pade.

Rate Slab / Price Range: Specific price brackets or value conditions ke आधार par alag-alag GST rate apply hona (e.g., footwear, apparel, or slab-based consumer goods).

Sample Classroom Slab Rate Structure

From (₹) To (₹) Practice GST Rate Central / State Split (Local)
0.00 1,000.00 5% CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5%
1,000.01 5,000.00 12% CGST 6% + SGST 6%
5,000.01 Above 18% CGST 9% + SGST 9%
Critical Note on Boundary Testing:
Price slab define karte waqt overlapping ranges from bachein (e.g., ₹0–₹1,000 and ₹1,000.01–₹5,000Boundary values jaifrom ₹999, ₹1,000, ₹1,000.50, ₹5,000, and ₹5,001 ko test karna mandatory is.

Creating & Linking GST Classification in TallyPrime

Step A: Create GST Classification

  1. Go to Gateway of Tally → Create → GST Classification (or Alter → GST Classification).
  2. Enter Name: Training GST Price Slab.
  3. Define Slabs:
    • From 0 to 1,000 → Rate: 5%
    • From 1,001 to 5,000 → Rate: 12%
    • From 5,001 and Above → Rate: 18%
  4. Press Ctrl + A to save.

Step B: Link Classification to Stock Item

  1. Go to Gateway of Tally → Create / Alter → Stock Item (e.g., Smart Watch).
  2. Under GST Details, set GST Applicable: Applicable.
  3. Under GST Rate & Classification, select Training GST Price Slab.
  4. Press Ctrl + A to save.

Rate Determination Scenarios (Unit Price vs Total Value)

Price slab evaluation item ke Unit Selling Price par depend karti hai:

  • Cafrom 1 (Rate: ₹800): Falls in Slab 1 (≤ ₹1,000) → GST @ 5% (CGST ₹20 + SGST ₹20) → Total: ₹840.
  • Cafrom 2 (Rate: ₹3,000): Falls in Slab 2 (₹1,001–₹5,000) → GST @ 12% (CGST ₹180 + SGST ₹180) → Total: ₹3,360.
  • Cafrom 3 (Rate: ₹7,000): Falls in Slab 3 (> ₹5,000) → GST @ 18% (CGST ₹630 + SGST ₹630) → Total: ₹8,260.
  • Cafrom 4 (Bulk Quantity: 10 Nos @ ₹900 = ₹9,000): Becaufrom Unit Price is ₹900, the applicable slab is 5% (not 18%Taxable Value = ₹9,000, Total GST = ₹450 (CGST ₹225 + SGST ₹225), Invoice Total = ₹9,450.

Verification Reports, Common Mistakes & FAQs

Verification Steps in TallyPrime:

  • Tax Analysis: Voucher entry ke dauran press Ctrl + O → GST - Tax Analysis (Alt + F5 for detail) to check if the correct slab was picked.
  • Sales Register: Check overall item values and taxes.
  • GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B: Verify outward taxable supplies and tax rate breakdowns under Table 4, 5, or 6.

Common Mistakes:

  • Confusing Unit Selling Price with Total Invoice Value when calculating slab thresholds.
  • Overlapping slab ranges (e.g., 0-1000 and 1000-5000) leading to tax rate ambiguity.
  • Creating GST Classification but forgetting to assign it to the Stock Item or Ledger.
  • Manually overriding the auto-calculated tax amount instead of checking master configurations.

Frequently Asked Questions:

  • Q: GST Classification kor hai?
    A: Centralized template jahan HSN, taxability, and rate slabs define kiye jaate hain taaki multiple items in uniform GST rules apply ho sakein.
  • Q: Agar price ₹1,000 from kam ho toh 5% and zyada ho toh 12% kaifrom set hoga?
    A: GST Classification in price range slabs (0 to 1,000 @ 5% and 1,000.01 to 5,000 @ 12%) define karke item ko classify kior jata is.
  • Q: Bulk quantity sale hone par rate total amount par lagta hai or per unit rate par?
    A: Standard item classifications item ke unit rate par evaluate isn, chahe quantity kitni also ho.
