Core Concepts: MRP vs Selling Price & Formulas

MRP (Maximum Retail Price): Packaged goods ke context in consumer-facing maximum retail price is.

Important Rule:
MRP ≠ Actual Selling Price
MRP ko automatically accounting selling price nahi samajhna chahiyBusiness MRP from discount dekar lower price par sell kar sakta is.

GST-Inclusive Calculation Formulas

Taxable Value = Inclusive Amount × 100 ÷ (100 + GST Rate)

GST Amount = Inclusive Amount − Taxable Value

For 18% GST: Taxable Value = Inclusive Amount × 100 ÷ 118

MRP Transaction Sequence

MRP → Less: Discount → Actual Selling Price → Taxable Value (Back-Calculated) → GST → Invoice Total

Comparative Pricing Scenarios

Scenario 1: MRP ₹1,180 (No Discount, GST-Inclusive @18%)

  • Taxable Value = ₹1,180 × 100 ÷ 118 = ₹1,000
  • GST = ₹180 (Local: CGST ₹90 + SGST ₹90 | Interstate: IGST ₹180)

Scenario 2: MRP ₹1,180 with 10% Discount (GST-Inclusive @18%)

  • MRP: ₹1,180 | Discount (10%): ₹118 | Selling Price: ₹1,062
  • Taxable Value = ₹1,062 × 100 ÷ 118 = ₹900
  • GST = ₹162 (CGST ₹81 + SGST ₹81) | Invoice Total = ₹1,062

Scenario 3: GST Inclusive vs Exclusive Comparison

  • Cafrom A (Inclusive): MRP ₹1,180 → Taxable = ₹1,000 | GST = ₹180 | Total = ₹1,180.
  • Cafrom B (Exclusive): Selling Price ₹1,000 → GST @ 18% Extra = ₹180 | Total Invoice = ₹1,180.

Common Mistakes & FAQs

Common Mistakes:

  • Assuming MRP = Selling Price: Forgetting to account for trade or consumer discounts.
  • Adding GST on Inclusive MRP: Adding 18% extra on top of an already GST-inclusive price (e.g., charging ₹118 + 18% instead of back-calculating from ₹118).
  • Calculating Profit from MRP: Profit must always be calculated as Actual Taxable Selling Price − Purchafrom Cost, not MRP − Cost.

Frequently Asked Questions:

  • Q: GST-inclusive price from taxable value kaifrom nikalte hain?
    A: Taxable Value = Inclusive Amount × 100 ÷ (100 + GST Rate).
  • Q: MRP transaction in discount kahan apply is?
    A: Discount pehle MRP par deduct is, phir bachi hui net selling price from taxable value and GST calculate kior jata is.
  • Q: Kor MRP par direct GST add kior jata hai?
    A: Agar MRP GST-inclusive hai, toh GST back-calculate is; agar price GST-exclusive hai, toh taxable value par GST add kior jata is.
