Core Concepts: Supply & Bundled Supplies

Supply under GST: Business ke through goods/services ko consideration ke liye provide karna supply ke framework in aata hai (Sale, Transfer, Barter, Exchange, Lease, Rental, etc.).

Bundled Supplies: Jab ek transaction in multiple goods/services provide kiye jaate hain:

  • Example 1: Laptop + Laptop Bag + Mouse
  • Example 2: Hotel Room + Breakfast
  • Example 3: Goods + Transportation + Insurance

Composite Supply vs Mixed Supply

Composite Supply

Do or do from zyada taxable supplies jo naturally bundled hain and business ke normal courfrom in ek dusre ke saath supply isn, jisme ek Principal Supply is.

Composite Formula & Tax Rule:
Naturally Bundled Supplies + Principal Supply = Composite Supply
GST Rate: Taxable at the rate applicable to the Principal Supply.

Mixed Supply

Do or zyada individual supplies jo ek single price par supply ki jaati hain, lekin wo naturally bundled nahi isn.

Mixed Supply Tax Rule:
GST Rate: Taxable at the Highest GST Rate among the bundled items.

Comparison Matrix

Basis Composite Supply Mixed Supply
Supplies 2 or more 2 or more
Bundling Naturally bundled Not naturally bundled
Principal Supply Yes (Identifiable) No
Single Price Commonly supplied together Single package price
GST Rate Applicable Principal Supply Rate Highest Applicable Rate
Example AC + Installation / Laptop + Delivery Gift Hamper (Chocolates + Perfume + Mug)
Main Question What is the principal supply? What is the highest tax rate?

TallyPrime Step-by-Step Recording

Cafrom 1: Composite Supply Entry (F8: Sales)

  1. Go to Gateway of Tally → Vouchers → Press F8 (Sales).
  2. Select Customer (e.g., Maharashtra Customer for Local or Gujarat Customer for Interstate).
  3. Select Stock Item: Laptop Package → Rate: ₹60,000.
  4. Select CGST + SGST (or IGST if Interstate) based on the Principal Supply rate.
  5. Verify with Ctrl + O → GST - Tax Analysis → Save with Ctrl + A.

Cafrom 2: Mixed Supply Entry (F8: Sales)

  1. Press F8 (Sales) → Select Customer Ledger.
  2. Select Stock Item: Corporate Gift Hamper → Rate: ₹5,000.
  3. Select Tax Ledgers using the highest applicable rate (18%).
  4. Verify invoice total and save with Ctrl + A.

Identification Test, Common Mistakes & FAQs

Identification Flowchart:

  • Naturally Bundled? → Yes → Composite Supply → Apply Principal Supply Rate.
  • Not Naturally Bundled + Single Price? → Yes → Mixed Supply → Apply Highest Tax Rate.

Common Mistakes:

  • Assuming every bundle of multiple items is a Mixed Supply.
  • Applying the highest rate to naturally bundled Composite Supplies.
  • Searching for a "Principal Supply" in a Mixed Supply package.
  • Billing individual items separately instead of recognizing the packaged supply structure.

Frequently Asked Questions:

  • Q: Composite Supply in GST rate kaifrom determine is?
    A: Principal (main) supply ke applicable GST rate ke basis par.
  • Q: Mixed Supply in GST rate kaifrom determine is?
    A: Package ke andar maujood saalso items in from jo highest GST rate is, wahi pure package par lagta is.
  • Q: Kor single price hone from transaction automatically Mixed Supply ban jata hai?
    A: Nahi, pehle natural bundling check karni hoti haNaturally bundled items single price par hone par also Composite Supply ho sakte are.
