What is GST (Goods & Services Tax)?

GST = Goods and Services Tax

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is a comprehensive destination-based indirect tax levied on the supply of goods and services.

In Simple Terms:

When a business supplies taxable goods or services, applicable GST is collected from the recipient and remitted to the government in accordance with statutory compliance rules.

The fundamental objective of GST is to eliminate cascading taxes and unify indirect taxation under a single nationwide structure.

Simple Example

ABC Traders ne ₹10,000 ke taxable goods sell kiye and applicable GST rate 18% is.

GST: ₹10,000 × 18% = ₹1,800

For Intra-State supplies (within the same state):

  • CGST = ₹900
  • SGST = ₹900
  • Total recoverable from customer: ₹10,000 + ₹1,800 = ₹11,800

For Inter-State supplies (between different states):

  • IGST = ₹1,800
  • Total invoice amount: ₹11,800

3.1 CGST

CGST = Central Goods and Services Tax

When a taxable supply occurs within the same State or Union Territory, GST is equally bifurcated into CGST and SGST/UTGST.

SGST = State Goods and Services Tax

In intra-State taxable supplies, SGST applies concurrently alongside CGST.

  • GST = 18%
  • CGST = ₹1,800
  • SGST = ₹1,800
  • Total = ₹23,600

IGST

IGST = Integrated Goods and Services Tax

Generally, Inter-State supply in IGST apply is.

  • Seller = Maharashtra
  • Buyer = Gujarat
  • Taxable value = ₹20,000
  • GST rate = 18%

IGST: ₹20,000 × 18% = ₹3,600

Invoice value: ₹20,000 + ₹3,600 = ₹23,600

Practice Calculation

Assume GST rate = 18% for practice only.

Transaction A

Taxable Value = ₹10,000 | Transaction = Intra-State

Calculate: CGST, SGST, Total GST, Invoice Value

Answer:

  • GST = ₹10,000 × 18% = ₹1,800
  • CGST = ₹900
  • SGST = ₹900
  • Invoice Value = ₹10,000 + ₹1,800 = ₹11,800

Transaction B

Taxable Value = ₹25,000 | Transaction = Inter-State

Calculate: IGST, Invoice Value

Answer:

  • IGST = ₹25,000 × 18% = ₹4,500
  • Invoice Value = ₹25,000 + ₹4,500 = ₹29,500

FAQs — Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ 1 — TallyPrime mein GST activate kaise karte hain?
TallyPrime mein company open karke F11 → Enable Goods and Services Tax (GST) → Yes kiya ja sakta haUske baad required GST registration details configure ki jati hain.
FAQ 2 — GST activate karne ke liye GSTIN mandatory hai?
Business ke actual GST setup mein GST registration details, including GSTIN where applicable, correctly maintain karna important haTallyPrime mein GSTIN/UIN company GST details mein enter kiya ja sakta haPractice ke liye dummy data use kiya ja sakta hai.
FAQ 3 — GST enable karne ke baad kya hum immediately sale entry kar sakte hain?
GST enable karna first step haAccurate GST transaction ke liye relevant party, ledger, stock/service, HSN/SAC, GST rate aur transaction details bhi properly configured honi chahiye.
FAQ 4 — CGST aur SGST kab lagte hain?
Basic intra-State taxable supply mein generally CGST aur SGST/UTGST components apply hote hain.
FAQ 5 — IGST kab lagta hai?
Basic inter-State taxable supply mein generally IGST apply hota haLekin special transactions mein place-of-supply aur other GST rules check karna zaroori hai.
FAQ 6 — Kya GST rate company level par set kiya ja sakta hai?
TallyPrime mein GST rate/HSN-SAC details company level ke saath relevant masters mein bhi configure ki ja sakti haiTallyPrime GST hierarchy ke according rate details ka source configure karne ki flexibility deta hai.
FAQ 7 — Kya har party ka GSTIN enter karna zaroori hai?
Registered parties ke GST transactions mein party ki GST registration details correctly maintain karna important haTallyPrime party ledger mein registration type aur GSTIN/UIN maintain karne ka option deta hai.
FAQ 8 — Kya ek company mein multiple GSTIN ho sakte hain?
Current TallyPrime versions multiple GST registrations ko support karte hain, depending on the version/setuTallyHelp ke current documentation mein multiple GST registrations configure karne ka option documented hai.
FAQ 9 — GST activate karne ke baad GSTIN change kar sakte hain?
GST details alter ki ja sakti hain, lekin real business data mein GST registration changes ko carefully handle karna chahiye because existing transactions/reporting can be affected.
FAQ 10 — GST activation aur GST return filing same cheez hai?
NGST activation = TallyPrime mein GST-related accounting configuration enable karnGST return filing = applicable GST return data prepare/reconcile/file karnYe course ke later days mein separately cover hoga.
FAQ 11 — HSN aur SAC kya hain?
HSN goods ko classify karne ke liye use hota haSAC services ko classify karne ke liye use hota haTallyPrime mein relevant GST rate aur HSN/SAC details configure ki ja sakti hain.
FAQ 12 — Agar GST enabled hai to kya har transaction par GST automatically lagega?
Nahi, blindly nahGST calculation transaction ki nature, party details, item/service, GST applicability, rate, place of supply aur other applicable rules par depend karti hai.
