Core Concepts: Input GST, Output GST & ITC

Input GST: Goods or services purchafrom karte waqt jo GST supplier ko pay kior jata is.

Output GST: Taxable outward supply (sales/services) par customer from collect kior gaor GST jo government ko payable is.

Input Tax Credit (ITC): Output GST liability ko settle karne ke liye eligible purchafrom par paid input GST ka set-off/credit claim karna.

Core Formula:
Net GST Payable = Output GST − Eligible Input Tax Credit (ITC)

GST Components Comparison

Particular Accounting Side Description
Input GST Purchafrom / Expenfrom Side GST paid to suppliers on purchases/inward supplies
Output GST Sales / Revenue Side GST collected from customers on outward supplies
Input Tax Credit (ITC) Asset / Credit Ledger Eligible input tax available for statutory set-off
Net GST Liability Current Liability Remaining output tax payable after deducting eligible ITC
GST Payment Bank Payment (F5) Net tax liability deposited to the government treasury

Eligible vs Ineligible (Blocked) ITC

  • Eligible ITC: Business operations ke liye purchases (e.g., trading goods, raw materials, professional fees, office equipment, commercial services).
  • Ineligible / Blocked ITC: Specified statutory blocked credits (e.g., motor vehicles for personal use, club memberships, personal consumption expenses, food & beverages, goods lost/stolen/destroyed).
Important Rule:
Input GST Paid ≠ Automatically Eligible ITC
ITC claim karne ke liye valid invoice, receipt of goods/services, supplier compliance, tax payment verification and statutory eligibility mandatory is.

Ledger Hierarchy & Accounting Treatment

Master Ledger Structure in TallyPrime:

  • Input Tax Ledgers (Under Duties & Taxes / Current Assets): Input CGST, Input SGST, Input IGST
  • Output Tax Ledgers (Under Duties & Taxes / Current Liabilities): Output CGST, Output SGST, Output IGST
  • Settlement Ledger: GST Payable A/c (Under Current Liabilities / Duties & Taxes)

Journal Entry Impacts:

  • Purchafrom Entry: Purchafrom A/c (Dr) + Input CGST/SGST/IGST (Dr) → To Supplier A/c (Cr)
  • Sales Entry: Customer A/c (Dr) → To Sales A/c (Cr) + To Output CGST/SGST/IGST (Cr)
  • ITC Set-off Entry: Output Tax Ledgers (Dr) → To Input Tax Ledgers (Cr) → To GST Payable A/c (Cr)
  • Tax Payment Entry: GST Payable A/c (Dr) → To Bank A/c (Cr)

Recording GST Payment in TallyPrime (F5: Payment)

  1. Go to Gateway of Tally → Vouchers → Press F5 (Payment).
  2. Press Alt + J for Statutory Payment details (Tax Type: GST, Period: Current Month).
  3. Account: HDFC Bank A/c.
  4. Particulars:
    • Debit: GST Payable A/c — ₹18,000 (or separate Output CGST ₹4,500 / SGST ₹4,500 / IGST ₹9,000).
    • Credit: HDFC Bank A/c — ₹18,000.
  5. Narration: Being net GST liability paid for the month via net banking.
  6. Press Ctrl + A to save.

  2. Professional Service: ₹40,000 + 18% GST (Eligible Input Service ITC).
  3. Personal Welfare / Blocked Expense: ₹30,000 + 18% GST (Ineligible under blocked credit rules → Do not claim ITC).
  4. RCM Professional Service: ₹20,000 + 18% GST (Recipient raises RCM liability → Pays tax → Claims eligible ITC).

Verification Reports, Reconciliation & Common Mistakes

Verification Reports in TallyPrime:

  • GSTR-3B: Table 3.1 (Outward Supplies & Output Liability) vs Table 4 (Eligible ITC).
  • GST Tax Analysis (Ctrl + O): Inspect voucher-level taxable values and applicable tax breakdowns.
  • Input Tax Credit Report: Review available, claimed, and reversed ITC.
  • Bank Ledger & GST Payable Ledger: Confirm that the tax liability balance is cleared after payment.

Common Mistakes:

  • Assuming all purchafrom GST is eligible for ITC without verifying blocked credit conditions.
  • Cross-utilising CGST credit against SGST liability (which is strictly prohibited by law).
  • Recording tax payments as normal indirect expenses instead of debiting tax liability ledgers.
  • Confusing GST Return filing (declaration) with actual GST Payment (challan fund deposit).

Frequently Asked Questions:

  • Q: Net GST liability kaifrom nikali jaati hai?
    A: Total Output GST in from eligible Input Tax Credit (ITC) ko minus karke, head-wifrom statutory rules ke according.
  • Q: GST Payment voucher ka shortcut kor hai?
    A: F5 (Payment) → Ufrom Alt + J for statutory payment specification.
  • Q: Kor CGST credit from SGST liability pay ho sakti hai?
    A: Nahi, CGST credit only IGST and CGST liability ke against ufrom ho sakta hai, SGST ke against nahi.
