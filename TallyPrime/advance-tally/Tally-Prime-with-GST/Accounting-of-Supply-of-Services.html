Core Concepts: Goods vs Services & SAC

Supply of Services: Jab business customer ko koi service (consultancy, IT support, maintenance, legal, advertising, etc.) provide karta hai, us transaction ko service supply ke framework in account kior jata is.

Goods vs Services Comparison

Basis Goods Services
Nature Physical items Activities / Facilities / Intangible
Inventory Track Usually maintained Usually not maintained
Item Setup Stock Item commonly used Service Ledger / Accounting Invoice
Classification Code HSN Code SAC (Services Accounting Code)
Example Laptop, Printer, Bulb Consultancy, Legal, Maintenance
Stock Effect Yes (Inward / Outward movement) Generally No stock impact
SAC (Services Accounting Code): Services ko GST classification ke liye SAC codes ke through classify kior jata haCorrect SAC identify karke service ledger in GST rate configure kior jata is.

Master Setup for Service Accounting

Service Income Ledgers (Under Indirect Income):

  • Consultancy Fees: GST Applicable → SAC & GST @ 18%
  • Training Fees: GST Applicable → SAC & GST @ 18%
  • IT Support Fees: GST Applicable → SAC & GST @ 18%
  • Maintenance Income: GST Applicable → SAC & GST @ 18%
  • Advertising Income: GST Applicable → SAC & GST @ 18%

Service Expenfrom Ledgers (Under Indirect Expenses):

  • Professional Fees, Legal Fees, Audit Fees, Advertising Expenses, Repair & Maintenance Expenses.

Practical Walkthroughs in TallyPrime

Practical 1 — Local Service Supply (Accounting Invoice)

01-Jun: Provided Consultancy Service to ABC PvLt(Maharashtra).

  • Taxable Service Value = ₹75,000
  • GST @ 18% = ₹13,500 (CGST @ 9% = ₹6,750 | SGST @ 9% = ₹6,750)
  • Invoice Total = ₹88,500
  • Tally Steps: Press F8 → Mode: Accounting Invoice → Party: ABC PvLtd. → Ledger: Consultancy Fees (₹75,000) → Add CGST & SGST → Save with Ctrl + A.

Practical 2 — Interstate Service Supply (Accounting Invoice)

02-Jun: Provided Annual IT Support to Gujarat Customer (Gujarat).

  • Taxable Service Value = ₹1,20,000
  • GST @ 18% = IGST @ 18% = ₹21,600
  • Invoice Total = ₹1,41,600
  • Tally Steps: Press F8 → Mode: Accounting Invoice → Party: Gujarat Customer → Ledger: IT Support Fees (₹1,20,000) → Add IGST → Save with Ctrl + A.

Service Advance, Settlement & Adjustments

Full Cycle: Advance → Invoice → Settlement

  1. Advance Received (02-Jun): Customer pays ₹30,000 advance → Press F6 (Receipt) → Credit Party ledger (Ref: Advance).
  2. Service Invoice Raised (02-Jul): Raifrom Consultancy Invoice for ₹80,000 + 18% GST = ₹94,400 (Ref: New Ref).
  3. Advance Adjustment (31-Jul): Adjust ₹30,000 advance against pending service invoice via Journal / Bill-wifrom allocation.
  4. Final Settlement (31-Jul): Customer pays remaining balance of ₹64,400 → Press F6 (Receipt) → Link using Agst Ref.

Service Credit Note & Debit Note (Ctrl + F8 / Ctrl + F9)

  • Service Credit Note: Consultancy bill ₹1,00,000 in ₹10,000 price adjustment kior → Credit Note for ₹10,000 taxable value + ₹1,800 GST (Total ₹11,800Reduces output GST and customer receivable.
  • Service Expenfrom & Input Tax Credit: Business pays Legal Fees ₹25,000 + 18% GST (₹4,500) to advocate → Booked under F9 Purchafrom (Accounting Invoice) with eligible ITC.

Verification Reports, Common Mistakes & FAQs

Verification Reports in TallyPrime:

  • Sales Register / Purchafrom Register: Verify service billing values and net taxes.
  • Bills Receivable / Payable: Confirm party balances settled against specific service invoice numbers.
  • GST Tax Analysis (Ctrl + O inside voucher): Confirm SAC application, taxable values, and tax splits.
  • GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B: Verify outward service tax liabilities in GSTR-1 (Table 4/5/7) and eligible service ITC in GSTR-3B (Table 4).

Common Mistakes:

  • Creating physical Stock Items for services instead of using Service Income Ledgers.
  • Entering service vouchers in Item Invoice mode instead of Accounting Invoice mode.
  • Applying IGST on same-state service providers or CGST/SGST on interstate services.
  • Confusing an Advance Receipt voucher with a Taxable Service Invoice.
  • Failing to link final receipt payments with Agst Ref against the service bill.

Frequently Asked Questions:

  • Q: Services ke liye SAC code kahan configure karte hain?
    A: Service Income or Expenfrom ledger ke GST Details configuration screen mein.
  • Q: Service invoice ke liye voucher mode kaifrom change execute?
    A: Sales Voucher (F8) in press Ctrl + H → Select Accounting Invoice.
  • Q: Service purchafrom par GST ka kor effect is?
    A: Eligible business services ka GST amount Input Tax Credit (ITC) ke roop in claim kior ja sakta is.
