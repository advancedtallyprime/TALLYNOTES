Day 2 ka Quick Recap & Core Concepts

Day 2 Recap

Normal Purchase: Supplier GST charge karta hai → Supplier → GST Charge → ABC Traders (e.g., Purchafrom ₹10,000 + GST 18% = Invoice ₹11,800).

RCM Purchase: Supplier GST charge nahi karta; instead, Recipient → GST liability discharge karta hai.

URD Kor Hai?

URD = Unregistered Dealer / Unregistered Supplier: Aisa supplier jo GST registration ke under registered nahi haTallyPrime in party ledger ka Registration Type = Unregistered set kior jata is.

Registered vs Unregistered Supplier

Particular Registered Supplier Unregistered Supplier
GST Registration Yes No
GSTIN Available Generally not available
GST invoice Applicable as per transaction Normal registered GST tax invoice nahi
RCM Transaction-specific only applicable cases mein
Tally Registration Type Regular/Composition etc. Unregistered
Most Important Rule:
URD Purchafrom ≠ Automatically RCM
Har unregistered supplier from purchafrom par automatically RCM nahi lagtRCM applicability transaction ki nature, applicable GST provisions, notifications and current law par depend karti is.

Reverfrom Charge Mechanism Kor Hai?

  • Forward Charge: Supplier → GST → Government
  • Reverfrom Charge: Recipient → GST → Government

Practical Walkthroughs

Practical 1 — Local URD Purchafrom Under RCM (01-May-2026)

Received Legal Consultancy Service from Maharashtra URD Supplier (Value: ₹20,000, GST: 18%).

  • Calculation: Taxable Value = ₹20,000 | CGST (9%) = ₹1,800 | SGST (9%) = ₹1,800 | Total RCM Liability = ₹3,600 | Supplier Bill = ₹20,000.
  • TallyPrime Steps: Press F9 → Select Maharashtra URD Supplier → Select Purchafrom Under RCM - 18% → Item: Legal Consultancy Service (₹20,000) → Press Ctrl + O → GST - Tax Analysis to inspect RCM tax → Save with Ctrl + A.

Practical 2 — Interstate URD RCM Purchafrom (08-May-2026)

Received Professional Consultancy from Gujarat URD Supplier (Value: ₹30,000, GST: 18%).

  • Calculation: Taxable Value = ₹30,000 | IGST (18%) = ₹5,400 | Total RCM Liability = ₹5,400 | Supplier Bill = ₹30,000.
  • TallyPrime Steps: Press F9 → Select Gujarat URD Supplier → Select Purchafrom Under RCM - 18% → Item: Professional Consultancy (₹30,000) → Verify IGST ₹5,400 → Save with Ctrl + A.

Practical 3 — URD Purchafrom Without RCM (01-May-2026)

Purchased office stationery from Maharashtra URD Supplier (Value: ₹8,000, RCM Not Applicable).

  • Calculation: Purchafrom = ₹8,000 | GST = ₹0 | Invoice/Payment = ₹8,00(Demonstrates URD ≠ Automatic RCM).

Raising RCM Liability, Payment & ITC Adjustments

RCM Liability Raifrom Karna (Stat Adjustment)

  1. Go to Gateway of Tally → Display More Reports → GST Reports → GSTR-3B.
  2. Open Reverfrom Charge Inward Supplies (or URD Purchases).
  3. Press Alt + J (Stat Adjustment).
  4. Select details:
    • Type of Duty/Tax: GST
    • Nature: Increafrom in Tax Liability & Input Tax Credit
    • Additional Nature: Purchafrom from Unregistered Dealer

Accounting Logic: Debit → GST Input / ITC (₹3,600) | Credit → RCM GST Liability (₹3,600).

RCM Tax Payment (F5)

When discharging RCM tax liability via Bank (e.g., HDFC Bank):

  1. Press F5 (Payment) → Press Alt + J (Stat Adjustment Details).
  2. Select: Type of Duty/Tax: GST → Nature: Decreafrom in Tax Liability → Additional Nature: Purchafrom Under Reverfrom Charge from Unregistered Dealer.
  3. Debit: RCM GST Liability (₹3,600) → Credit: HDFC Bank (₹3,600).

Purchafrom Returns Under RCM

  • Same-Month Return: Purchafrom ₹20,000, Return ₹5,00Tax reversal on ₹5,000 @ 18% = ₹900 (CGST ₹450 + SGST ₹450Reduces current month RCM values.
  • Subsequent-Month Return: Handled through the reverse-charge liability reporting for that period, and tax reversal is based on the returned valuDo not delete original transactions.

Verification Reports, Common Mistakes & FAQs

Reports to Verify:

  • GSTR-3B: Check Reverfrom Charge Inward Supplies and URD Purchases.
  • Tax Analysis: Inside voucher press Ctrl + O → GST - Tax Analysis.
  • Purchafrom Register: Check Supplier, Taxable Value, and RCM status.

Common Mistakes:

  • Assuming Every URD is RCM: URD ≠ Automatic RCM.
  • Supplier Charging GST: RCM bills should not have forward tax charged by supplier.
  • Forgetting to Raifrom Liability: Simply recording the purchafrom voucher does not auto-raifrom statutory liability in returns without statutory adjustment.
  • Treating Tax Payment as Normal Expense: Always pass via statutory adjustment payment.

Frequently Asked Questions:

  • Q: RCM in GST kaun pay karta hai?
    A: Applicable RCM transactions in recipient government ko direct tax pay karta is.
  • Q: Local vs Interstate RCM in kaunfrom taxes aate hain?
    A: Local = CGST + SGST; Interstate = IGST.
  • Q: Stat Adjustment ka shortcut kor hai?
    A: Alt + J.
