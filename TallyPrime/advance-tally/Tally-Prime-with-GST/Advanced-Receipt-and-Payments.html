Learning Objectives

Is class ke end tak student:

  • Receipt aur payment ka accounting concept samjhega.
  • Normal receipt aur advance receipt mein difference identify karega.
  • Customer advance ka accounting treatment samjhega.
  • GST advance receipt ka basic concept samjhega.
  • Same-period aur subsequent-period advance scenarios handle karega.
  • TallyPrime mein GST advance receipt record kar payega.
  • Sales invoice ke against advance adjust kar payega.
  • Partial advance adjustment kar payega.
  • Multiple advances ko ek invoice ke against adjust kar payega.
  • Cancelled order ka advance refund record kar payega.
  • Applicable GST liability ko increase/decrease kar payega.
  • Supplier advance payment record kar payega.
  • Advance adjustment ke baad customer/supplier outstanding verify kar payega.
  • GST reports mein advance-related transactions verify kar payega.
  • Common advance accounting errors identify kar payega.

Core Concepts: Normal Receipt vs Advance Receipt

Receipt (F6): Business ko cash ya bank ke through incoming funds receive hona.

Payment (F5): Business se suppliers, expenses, refunds, ya advances ke liye outgoing funds pay hona.

Comparison Matrix

Particular Normal Receipt Advance Receipt
Invoice Available Yes Usually No
Money Received Against existing pending invoice Before / against future supply
Customer Balance Outstanding balance reduces Advance balance / liability created
Bill-wise Treatment Against Reference (Agst Ref) Advance (Advance Ref)
GST Treatment Invoice-based reporting Subject to supply type & applicable GST rules
Example ₹59,000 pending bill payment ₹10,000 token before order delivery
Golden Rule:
Advance Receipt ≠ Sales Invoice
Advance received hone par sales create nahi hotCustomer advance business ke liye Current Liability hoti hai jab tak final supply complete na ho jaye.

Customer Advance vs Supplier Advance

Particular Customer Advance Supplier Advance
Money Movement Inward (Incoming Funds) Outward (Outgoing Funds)
Voucher Type Receipt (F6) Payment (F5)
Accounting Classification Current Liabilities Current Assets (Loans & Advances)
Later Adjustment Adjusted in Sales Invoice (F8) Adjusted in Purchase Invoice (F9)

Company & Masters Configuration

Company Setup

  • Company: ABC Traders (Maharashtra) | Registration: Regular | FY: 2026–27

Required Masters to Create:

  • Customer (Local): ABC Enterprises (Under Sundry Debtors, State: Maharashtra)
  • Customer (Interstate): Gujarat Enterprises (Under Sundry Debtors, State: Gujarat)
  • Customer Advance Ledger: Customer Advance (Under Current Liabilities)
  • Supplier: ABC Suppliers (Under Sundry Creditors)
  • Sales Ledger: Sales – 18% (Under Sales Accounts)
  • Tax Ledgers: CGST, SGST, IGST (Under Duties & Taxes)
  • Stock Item: Office Chair (Unit: Nos, GST @ 18%, Opening: 50 Nos @ ₹2,000)

Advance Calculation Formula (Classroom Practice @ 18% GST)

Advance Amount Received: ₹10,000
Taxable Value: ₹10,000 ÷ 1.18 = ₹8,474.58
CGST (9%): ₹8,474.58 × 9% = ₹762.71
SGST (9%): ₹8,474.58 × 9% = ₹762.71
Total GST Component: ₹1,525.42

Practical Walkthroughs in TallyPrime

Practical 1: Advance Received & Sale Made in Same GST Period

  1. 01-Apr (Advance Receipt): Press F6 (Receipt) → Select Cash/Bank → Party: ABC Enterprises → Bill-wise: Advance (Ref: ADV-01, ₹10,000Use Alt + J for Stat Adjustment if applicable.
  2. 01-Apr (Sales Invoice): Press F8 (Sales) → Sell 20 Nos Office Chairs @ ₹500 = ₹10,000 taxable + 18% GST (CGST ₹900 + SGST ₹900) = Total Invoice ₹11,800.
  3. Advance Adjustment: Bill-wise details pop-up mein select Agst Ref → Pick ADV-01 → Adjust ₹10,00Balance remaining on bill = ₹1,800 (New Ref).
  4. Final Settlement: Customer pays balance ₹1,800 → Press F6 (Receipt) → Select Agst Ref → Customer outstanding becomes Nil.

Practical 2: Advance Received in One Month, Sale in Next Month

  • 31-May (Advance): Record ₹10,000 advance receipt in MaRaise tax liability via Stat Adjustment (Alt + J → Increase in Tax Liability → Advance Receipt): Tax on Advance Dr ₹1,525.42 to CGST Cr ₹762.71 & SGST Cr ₹762.71.
  • 05-Jun (Sales Invoice): Record June sales invoice of ₹11,800 and adjust ₹10,000 against advance.
  • Tax Adjustment: Reverse/adjust earlier advance liability via Stat Adjustment (Alt + J → Decrease in Tax Liability → Sales Against Advance Receipts): CGST Dr ₹762.71 & SGST Dr ₹762.71 to Tax on Advance Cr ₹1,525.42.

Practical 3: Advance Received but Order Cancelled (Refund)

  1. Advance Received: Record ₹10,000 advance receipt and applicable tax liability.
  2. Refund Payment (F5): Press F5 (Payment) → Select Refund Against Advance Receipt → Pick original advance reference → Pay ₹10,000 from Bank.
  3. GST Liability Reversal: Stat Adjustment (Alt + J → Decrease in Tax Liability → Cancellation of Advance Receipts): Debit CGST ₹762.71 & SGST ₹762.71, Credit Tax on Advance ₹1,525.42.

Practical 4: Partial & Multiple Advance Adjustments

  • Partial Adjustment: Advance received ₹50,00Final invoice ₹1,18,00Adjust ₹50,000 (Agst Ref) → Balance receivable ₹68,000 collected via normal receipt.
  • Multiple Advances: Advance 1 (₹20,000) + Advance 2 (₹30,000) = ₹50,000 total advancIn final sales invoice of ₹1,18,000, link both advance references under Agst Ref → Balance due = ₹68,000.

Practical 5: Supplier Advance Payment

05-Jul: Paid ₹40,000 advance to ABC Suppliers via Bank (F5: Payment, Ref: Advance).

02-Jul: Purchase invoice of ₹1,00,000 + 18% GST = ₹1,18,000 (F9: PurchaseIn bill-wise details, select Agst Ref to adjust ₹40,000 advancBalance payable = ₹78,000.

Verification Reports, Common Mistakes & FAQs

Verification Reports in TallyPrime:

  • Customer Ledger & Day Book: Verify chronological posting: Advance Receipt → Sales Invoice → Advance Adjustment → Final Receipt.
  • Bill-wise Outstanding: Ensure pending advance balances clear out to Nil once final settlements are recorded.
  • GST Tax Analysis (Ctrl + O inside voucher): Confirm tax breakdown on taxable values.
  • GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B: Verify Table 11 (Advances Received / Adjusted) in GSTR-1 and tax adjustments in GSTR-3B.

Common Mistakes:

  • Treating customer advances as direct sales revenue in the Profit & Loss statement.
  • Failing to link advances in sales invoices using Agst Ref, creating double-counted receivables.
  • Recording supplier advances as indirect business expenses instead of Current Assets.
  • Refunding an advance via payment voucher without linking the original advance reference.
  • Forgetting to reverse statutory advance tax liabilities upon order cancellations.

Frequently Asked Questions:

  • Q: Customer Advance kis group ke under banta hai?
    A: Current Liabilities (kyunki goods/services deliver hone tak yeh business ki liability hoti hai).
  • Q: Supplier Advance kis group ke under banta hai?
    A: Current Assets (Loans & Advances).
  • Q: Bill-wise allocation mein advance kaise adjust hota hai?
    A: Sales Invoice ke dauran Bill-wise screen par Agst Ref select karke respective advance reference number ko choose kiya jata hai.

Quick Revision & Completion Checklist

Day 4 Golden Workflow:
Advance Received (F6) → Statutory Tax Treatment (if applicable) → Sales Invoice (F8) → Bill-wise Advance Adjustment (Agst Ref) → Balance Receipt (F6) → GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B Reconciliation

Completion Checklist:

  • Normal Receipt vs Advance Receipt differentiated
  • Customer Advance (Current Liabilities) and Supplier Advance (Current Assets) configured
  • Same-month and subsequent-month advance adjustments practiced
  • Order cancellation and advance refund workflows executed
  • Partial and multiple advance set-offs completed
  • Bill-wise Outstanding, GST Tax Analysis, and GSTR-1/GSTR-3B reports verified
