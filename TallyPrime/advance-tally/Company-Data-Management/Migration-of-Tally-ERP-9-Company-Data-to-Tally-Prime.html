1. Core Concept

Upgrading from legacy Tally.ERP 9 to TallyPrime requires a data migration process. TallyPrime utilizes an upgraded database architecture and enhanced statutory algorithms. The migration tool converts ERP 9 data folders seamlessly without loss of accounting or tax records.

2. Pre-Migration Checklist

  • Complete Data Backup: Take a mandatory full backup of the Tally.ERP 9 data directory.
  • Resolve Unreconciled BRS: Reconcile open bank entries where possible.
  • Finalize Open Statutory Mismatches: Resolve pending GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B triangulations.
  • Administrator Login: Ensure you possess the company administrator username and password.

3. Step-by-Step Migration Process in TallyPrime

Navigation: Launch TallyPrime → Select Company screen → Point to ERP 9 Data path.
  1. In TallyPrime, click Select from drive and browse to your Tally.ERP 9 data directory.
  2. Companies requiring conversion will display a status label: Migration Required.
  3. Select the company and press Enter.
  4. TallyPrime displays the Migrate Company Data confirmation screen.
  5. Set Backup company data before migration? to Yes.
  6. Click or press R (Migrate).
  7. TallyPrime processes master files, recalculates tax ledgers, upgrades voucher schema, and opens the company ready for use.

4. Post-Migration Verification

  1. Compare the Trial Balance totals between Tally.ERP 9 and TallyPrime to verify 100% debit/credit match.
  2. Open Balance Sheet and verify Closing Stock and Capital balance.
  3. Verify GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B tax liability summaries for the current period.

5. Interview Questions & Answers

Q1. Can a migrated TallyPrime company data folder be reopened in older Tally.ERP 9?

Ans: No. Once company data is migrated to TallyPrime's upgraded database architecture, it is forward-compatible only and cannot be opened in Tally.ERP 9. Hence, retaining the pre-migration backup is critical.
